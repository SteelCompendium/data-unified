> ⭐️ **Soot Strike**

> ⭐️ **Ashen Cloud** 1 Essence
>

An enemy can't establish [line of effect](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/rule.combat/line-of-effect) beyond the ash while occupying an affected square.
