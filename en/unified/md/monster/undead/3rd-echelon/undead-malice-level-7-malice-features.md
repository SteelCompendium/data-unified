At the start of any level 7 or higher undead's turn, you can spend [Malice](scc.v1:mcdm.monsters.v1/rule.monster/malice) to activate one of the following features.

> ⭐️ **Prior Malice Features (2-7+ [Malice](scc.v1:mcdm.monsters.v1/rule.monster/malice))**
>
> The undead activates a [Malice](scc.v1:mcdm.monsters.v1/rule.monster/malice) feature available to undead of level 6 or lower.

> 🌀 **Necrotic Rupture (5 [Malice](scc.v1:mcdm.monsters.v1/rule.monster/malice))**
>
> Until the end of the round, whenever an undead is reduced to 0 [Stamina](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/rule.health/stamina), they deal 8 corruption damage to each enemy within 3 squares of them.
