*A hundred silver?! An insult! I turned my father in for fifty golden crowns. And he was innocent!*

**Prerequisite:** You are declared an outlaw by a governmental authority.

**Effect:** Choose one of the following benefits:

- *Honor Among Thieves:* When negotiating with criminals, your [Renown](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/rule.resource/renown) score is considered to be 2 higher than usual.
- *Minion Mower:* When you make a [melee](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/rule.combat/melee) [strike](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/rule.combat/strike) that targets a minion and at least one more minion is within [distance](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/rule.combat/distance) of the [strike](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/rule.combat/strike), the [strike](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/rule.combat/strike) gains a +3 damage [bonus](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/rule.dice/bonuses-and-penalties).
- *No, You're Under Arrest!:* You gain an [edge](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/rule.dice/edge) on the [Escape Grab](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/feature.common.maneuvers/escape-grab) maneuver. Additionally, when you succeed on a [test](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/rule.test/test) to escape bonds or manacles, as part of the same maneuver, you can transfer the bonds or manacles to an [adjacent](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/rule.combat/adjacent) creature of the same [size](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/rule.character/size) without them immediately noticing.
