*The sword Vanartha has been remade, mightier now than on the day it was forged.*

**Prerequisite:** You complete a downtime project to imbue armor, an [implement](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/rule.treasure/implement), or a weapon with a 9th-level [enhancement](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/rule.treasure/enhancement).

**Effect:** Choose one of the following benefits:

- *Masterpiece:* The armor, [implement](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/rule.treasure/implement), or weapon can be imbued a fourth time, with any [enhancement](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/rule.treasure/enhancement) the item qualifies for.
- *Research Dividends:* You gain the [item prerequisite](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/rule.downtime/item-prerequisite) for an armor, [implement](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/rule.treasure/implement), or weapon [enhancement](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/rule.treasure/enhancement) of your choice. Additionally, you learn the [project source](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/rule.downtime/project-source) language for that [enhancement](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/rule.treasure/enhancement).
- *Skilled Hands:* You have a skill from the [crafting skill group](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/skill.group/crafting) that would have been used during the creation of the prerequisite item. If you already have that skill, you instead gain an [edge](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/rule.dice/edge) on [tests](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/rule.test/test) made using the skill. Additionally, you gain a second skill of your choice from the [crafting skill group](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/skill.group/crafting).
- *Strong Hands Make Light Work:* Whenever you make a [project roll](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/rule.downtime/project-roll), you can use [Might](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/rule.character/might) as the [project roll](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/rule.downtime/project-roll) [characteristic](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/rule.character/characteristic).
