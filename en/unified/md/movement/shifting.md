[Shifting](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/movement/shifting) is a careful form of movement that allows a creature to move safely past dangerous foes. Certain abilities, features, and other rules allow you to [shift](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/movement/shifting) a specific number of squares, sometimes up to your [speed](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/rule.character/speed). Whenever you [shift](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/movement/shifting), creatures can't make [opportunity attacks](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/rule.combat/opportunity-attack) against you triggered by that movement (see [Opportunity Attacks](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/rule.combat/opportunity-attack) later in this chapter).

You can't [shift](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/movement/shifting) into or while within [difficult terrain](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/movement/difficult-terrain) or [damaging terrain](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/movement/damaging-terrain) (see below). If a rule allows you to [shift](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/movement/shifting), you can choose to instead move up to the number of squares you would have shifted (for example, to get out of [difficult terrain](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/movement/difficult-terrain)). However, you can't combine moving and [shifting](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/movement/shifting) within that movement.
