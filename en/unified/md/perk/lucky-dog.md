Whenever you fail a [test](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/rule.test/test) using any skill from the [intrigue skill group](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/skill.group/intrigue), you can lose [Stamina](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/rule.health/stamina) equal to 1d6 + your level to improve the outcome of the [test](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/rule.test/test) by one tier. You can use this perk only once per [test](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/rule.test/test).
