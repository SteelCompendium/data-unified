*You focus your connection to the Primordial Chaos into a seething storm.*

| **Area, Magic** |                     **[Maneuver](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/rule.combat/turn)** |
|-----------------|---------------------------------:|
| **📏 3 [aura](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/rule.combat/aura)**   | **🎯 Each creature in the area** |

**Effect:** Until the end of the encounter or until you are [dying](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/rule.health/dying), each enemy target takes damage of your primordial [damage type](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/rule.damage/damage-type) equal to twice your [Might](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/rule.character/might) score at the end of each of your [turns](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/rule.combat/turn). Additionally, you can [fly](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/movement/fly) while the [aura](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/rule.combat/aura) is active. Each ally target who starts or ends their [turn](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/rule.combat/turn) in the area can also [fly](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/movement/fly) until the start of their next [turn](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/rule.combat/turn) or until the effect ends.

**Special:** When you use this ability outside of combat without spending ferocity, you must spend 1 uninterrupted minute summoning a [primordial storm](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/feature.fury.stormwight-kits/primordial-storm) that fills the area, and you take 1d6 damage before the ability takes effect. The storm lasts for 1 hour or until a combat encounter begins.
