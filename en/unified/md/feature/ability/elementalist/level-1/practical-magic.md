*Your mastery of elemental power lets you customize your conjurations.*

| **Magic**              | **Maneuver** |
|------------------------|-------------:|
| **📏 Self; see below** |  **🎯 Self** |

**Effect:** Choose one of the following effects:

- You use the [Knockback](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/feature.common.maneuvers/knockback) maneuver (see Chapter 10: [Combat](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/chapter/combat)), but its [distance](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/rule.combat/distance) becomes the range of your [Hurl Element](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/feature.ability.elementalist.level-1/hurl-element) ability, and you use [Reason](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/rule.character/reason) instead of [Might](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/rule.character/might) for the [power roll](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/rule.dice/power-roll).
- You choose a creature within the [distance](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/rule.combat/distance) of your [Hurl Element](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/feature.ability.elementalist.level-1/hurl-element) ability and one of the following [damage types](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/rule.damage/damage-type): acid, cold, corruption, fire, lightning, poison, or sonic. That creature takes damage of the chosen type equal to your [Reason](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/rule.character/reason) score.
- You [teleport](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/movement/teleport) up to a number of squares equal to your [Reason](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/rule.character/reason) score. If you choose this option, you can spend essence to [teleport](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/movement/teleport) 1 additional square for each essence spent.
