You have a double [bane](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/rule.dice/bane) on [ability rolls](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/rule.dice/ability-roll) made against the target while they are [slowed](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/condition/slowed) this way.
