Whenever you obtain a success on a [test](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/rule.test/test) to interact with an [NPC](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/rule.general/npc) using a skill from the [interpersonal group](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/skill.group/interpersonal), you can form a bond with that [NPC](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/rule.general/npc). When you enter into a negotiation with a bonded [NPC](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/rule.general/npc), their patience increases by 1 (to a maximum of 5). Additionally, the first time during a negotiation that you personally make an argument that would increase a bonded [NPC](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/rule.general/npc)'s interest by 1, you instead increase their interest by 2 (to a maximum of 5).

You can have a number of bonds active equal to your level. When you form a bond with a new [NPC](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/rule.general/npc) that would exceed the limit, you must choose which of your active bonds to lose.
