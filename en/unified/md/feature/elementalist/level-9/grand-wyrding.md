You have mastered the magic of shaping a wyrd, and can use your [Wyrding](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/feature.elementalist.level-6/wyrding) feature as a main action.

Additionally, when you have 5 or more [Victories](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/rule.resource/victories), choose one of the following [damage types](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/rule.damage/damage-type): acid, cold, corruption, fire, lightning, poison, or sonic. You have immunity all to that type.
