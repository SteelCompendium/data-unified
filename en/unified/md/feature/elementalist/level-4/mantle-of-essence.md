While you have 3 or more essence and are not [dying](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/rule.health/dying), you exude an aura of magic whose [distance](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/rule.combat/distance) is equal to your [Reason](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/rule.character/reason) score. The effects within the area of the [aura](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/rule.combat/aura) are based on your specialization, as shown on the [Mantle of Essence](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/feature.elementalist.level-4/mantle-of-essence) Specialization Effects table. You can activate and deactivate the [aura](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/rule.combat/aura) at will (no action required).

| Specialization | Feature         |
|----------------|-----------------|
| Earth          | [Quaking Earth](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/feature.elementalist.level-4/quaking-earth)   |
| Fire           | [Burning Grounds](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/feature.elementalist.level-4/burning-grounds) |
| Green          | [Flowering Bed](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/feature.elementalist.level-4/flowering-bed)   |
| Void           | [Veiling Bed](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/feature.elementalist.level-4/veiling-bed)     |
