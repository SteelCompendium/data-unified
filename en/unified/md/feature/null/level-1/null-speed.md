The flow of psionic power through you allows you to achieve high velocity. You gain a [bonus](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/rule.dice/bonuses-and-penalties) to [speed](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/rule.character/speed) and to the number of squares you can [shift](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/movement/shifting) when you take the [Disengage](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/feature.common.move-actions/disengage) move action equal to your [Agility](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/rule.character/agility) score.
