Whenever you use your [Inertial Shield](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/feature.ability.null.level-1/inertial-shield) [triggered action](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/rule.combat/triggered-action), you gain immunity to acid, cold, corruption, fire, lightning, poison, and sonic damage equal to your [Intuition](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/rule.character/intuition) score against the triggering damage.
