Your expertise with weapons has grown to true mastery. Whenever you use a [signature ability](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/rule.combat/signature-ability) from one of your equipped kits or make a [free strike](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/feature.common.main-actions/free-strike) using a weapon from one of your equipped kits, you can negate a [bane](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/rule.dice/bane) on the [power roll](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/rule.dice/power-roll) or reduce a double [bane](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/rule.dice/bane) to a [bane](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/rule.dice/bane).
