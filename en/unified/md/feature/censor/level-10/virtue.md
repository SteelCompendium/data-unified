You have an epic resource called [virtue](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/feature.censor.level-10/virtue). Each time you finish a [respite](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/rule.resource/respite), you gain [virtue](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/feature.censor.level-10/virtue) equal to the [XP](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/rule.resource/experience) you gain. You can spend [virtue](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/feature.censor.level-10/virtue) on your abilities as if it were wrath.

Additionally, you can spend 3 [virtue](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/feature.censor.level-10/virtue) to access one of your deity's domains that you usually don't have access to. When you do, you can use that domain's features until you finish another [respite](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/rule.resource/respite).

[Virtue](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/feature.censor.level-10/virtue) remains until you spend it.
