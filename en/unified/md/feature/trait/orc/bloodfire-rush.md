The magic coursing through your veins makes you run faster in the heat of battle. The first time in any [combat round](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/rule.combat/combat-round) that you take damage, you gain a +2 [bonus](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/rule.dice/bonuses-and-penalties) to [speed](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/rule.character/speed) until the end of the round.
