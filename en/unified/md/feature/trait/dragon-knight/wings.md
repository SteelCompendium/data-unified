You possess wings powerful enough to take you airborne. While using your wings to [fly](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/movement/fly), you can stay aloft for a number of rounds equal to your [Might](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/rule.character/might) score (minimum 1 round) before you fall. While using your wings to [fly](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/movement/fly) at 3rd level or lower, you have [damage weakness](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/rule.damage/damage-weakness) 5.
