You gain an [edge](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/rule.dice/edge) on [tests](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/rule.test/test) made to hide and sneak. Additionally, whenever you fall, you can use a free [triggered action](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/rule.combat/triggered-action) to use your [Aspect of the Wild](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/feature.ability.fury.stormwight-kits/aspect-of-the-wild) ability.
