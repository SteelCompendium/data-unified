If a creature is fully submerged in water, they have fire immunity 5 and lightning weakness 5. If they can't automatically swim at full [speed](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/rule.character/speed) while moving, their [power rolls](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/rule.dice/power-roll) take a [bane](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/rule.dice/bane).
