Defenders are tough creatures able to take a lot of damage, and who can force enemies to attack them instead of squishier targets. Defenders often act in [squads](scc.v1:mcdm.monsters.v1/rule.monster/squad) with allies who have lower [Stamina](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/rule.health/stamina), such as [controllers](scc.v1:mcdm.monsters.v1/rule.role/controller) and [hexers](scc.v1:mcdm.monsters.v1/rule.role/hexer).
