[Victories](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/rule.resource/victories) temporarily increase a hero's power during an adventure, but Experience (abbreviated "XP") permanently improves their capabilities. Each time you finish a [respite](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/rule.resource/respite) (see below), you gain XP equal to your [Victories](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/rule.resource/victories), then your [Victories](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/rule.resource/victories) reset to 0. In other words, your [Victories](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/rule.resource/victories) are converted to XP when you finish a [respite](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/rule.resource/respite).

For more information on how XP increases your hero's power, see Heroic Advancement in [Making a Hero](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/chapter/making-a-hero).
