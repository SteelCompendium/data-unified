A creature who has "[fly](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/movement/fly)" in their [speed](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/rule.character/speed) entry, or who gains the temporary ability to [fly](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/movement/fly), can move through the air vertically or horizontally at full [speed](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/rule.character/speed) and remain in midair. If a [flying](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/movement/fly) creature is [knocked prone](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/condition/prone) or has their [speed](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/rule.character/speed) reduced to 0, they fall (see Falling below).
