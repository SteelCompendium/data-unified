When a creature [teleports](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/movement/teleport), they move from one space to another space instantaneously. The following rules apply to [teleporting](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/movement/teleport):

- [Teleporting](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/movement/teleport) doesn't provoke [opportunity attacks](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/rule.combat/opportunity-attack) or other effects that are triggered by a creature moving.
- When a creature [teleports](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/movement/teleport), they bypass any obstacles between the space they leave and their destination space.
- A creature [teleporting](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/movement/teleport) themself must have [line of effect](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/rule.combat/line-of-effect) to their destination space. A creature [teleporting](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/movement/teleport) another creature must have [line of effect](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/rule.combat/line-of-effect) from the space the [teleport](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/movement/teleport)ed creature leaves and to their destination space.
- A [teleporting](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/movement/teleport) creature's destination space can't be occupied by another creature or object.
- The effect that lets a creature [teleport](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/movement/teleport) indicates how far they can [teleport](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/movement/teleport). That [distance](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/rule.combat/distance) can be greater than the creature's [speed](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/rule.character/speed).
- If a creature can [teleport](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/movement/teleport) as part of their usual movement, they can use the [Advance](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/feature.common.move-actions/advance) move action to [teleport](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/movement/teleport) a number of squares up to their usual [speed](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/rule.character/speed), unmodified by [conditions](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/rule.combat/condition) or effects.
- If a creature [teleports](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/movement/teleport) [while prone](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/condition/prone), they can be standing when they reach their destination space provided they are able to stand. If a prone creature is [teleport](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/movement/teleport)ed by another creature, it is up to that creature whether the [teleport](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/movement/teleport)ed creature remains prone or stands if they are able.
- If you [teleport](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/movement/teleport) while affected by the [grabbed](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/condition/grabbed) or [restrained](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/condition/restrained) [conditions](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/rule.combat/condition), those [conditions](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/rule.combat/condition) end for you.
- When a creature [teleports](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/movement/teleport), they must leave the space where they start and enter a new space. A creature can't [teleport](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/movement/teleport) to and from the same space.
