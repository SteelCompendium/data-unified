Areas of acid, fire, sharp rocks, lava, or any other terrain that causes damage to creatures within it is [damaging terrain](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/movement/damaging-terrain). The damage dealt by [damaging terrain](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/movement/damaging-terrain) is noted in the terrain's description or in the description of the effect that creates the terrain.
