Maybe you were bitten as a child, or maybe it's a family curse. Either way, you have a malady that is best not discussed in public, lest torches and pitchforks make an appearance.

**Benefit:** Whenever you make a non-minion creature [winded](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/rule.health/winded) or kill a non-minion creature, you gain 1 [surge](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/rule.resource/surge).

**Drawback:** At the start of each of your [turns](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/rule.combat/turn), if you have five or more [surges](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/rule.resource/surge)—or one or more [surges](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/rule.resource/surge) while in moonlight—you lose all your [surges](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/rule.resource/surge) and become a wolfish hybrid until the end of your [turn](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/rule.combat/turn). While in that form, you have your usual statistics, but you must make a [melee](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/rule.combat/melee) [free strike](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/feature.common.main-actions/free-strike) against the nearest creature if you can. You can [shift](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/movement/shifting) up to your [speed](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/rule.character/speed) toward that creature if necessary. If allies and enemies are equally near, you target an ally.

**Special:** You can't take this complication if you are a [fury](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/class/fury) with the stormwight [primordial aspect](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/feature.fury.level-1/primordial-aspect).
