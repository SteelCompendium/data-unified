You used to be a con artist, but those days are pretty much behind you. Being a hero is an even better racket. After all, if you're saving the world, who can be mad at you for stealing a few coins along the way?

**Benefit:** You have one skill of your choice from the [intrigue skill group](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/skill.group/intrigue).

**Drawback:** Whenever you meet an [NPC](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/rule.general/npc) for the first time, the Director can decide that [NPC](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/rule.general/npc) was a victim of one of your previous cons and remembers you. If they do so, the party gains a [hero token](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/rule.resource/hero-token).
