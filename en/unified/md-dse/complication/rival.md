Whatever your accomplishments, you'll forever measure yourself against a former companion who always seemed to stay one step ahead of you.

**Benefit:** Choose one of your skills. That skill grants a +3 [bonus](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/rule.dice/bonuses-and-penalties) to [tests](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/rule.test/test) instead of +2.

**Drawback:** Your [rival](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/complication/rival) has similar statistics to yours, but always had one skill they excelled at, as determined by the Director. Intimidated by their prowess, you take a [bane](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/rule.dice/bane) on [tests](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/rule.test/test) using that skill.
