A deal you made went south, or you got involved with the wrong people. Now you owe a debt or a ransom that would bankrupt a minor noble. To pay it off, you'll need to take some dangerous risks.

**Benefit:** You're good with money—because you've had to be. Whenever you earn [Wealth](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/rule.resource/wealth), you earn 1 more than usual.

**Drawback:** Your starting [Wealth](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/rule.resource/wealth) is −5. While your [Wealth](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/rule.resource/wealth) is lower than 1, you can purchase items as if you had 1 [Wealth](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/rule.resource/wealth), but you're frequently visited by threatening creditors, and shopkeepers often lock their doors when they see you coming.
