Your personal idol was a mighty hero, and you have modeled yourself after them. From studying the many heroic tales told of them, you hope to someday learn your idol's most famous battle technique.

**Benefit:** Choose a [heroic ability](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/rule.general/heroic-ability) for your class of a higher level than you currently are. When you take this ability in future, its [Heroic Resource](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/rule.resource/heroic-resource) cost is permanently reduced by 2 (to a minimum of 1).

**Drawback:** In your quest for advanced techniques, you have neglected the basics. Choose a [heroic ability](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/rule.general/heroic-ability) you already know. That ability's [Heroic Resource](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/rule.resource/heroic-resource) cost is permanently increased by 1.
