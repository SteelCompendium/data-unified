At the edge of the world, you were lost in a winter storm and [presumed dead](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/title/presumed-dead). But an unknown fate or power kept you alive, bringing you back with frosty skin and pale eyes.

**Benefit:** You have cold immunity 5. Additionally, whenever you make a [strike](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/rule.combat/strike) that deals untyped damage, that [strike](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/rule.combat/strike) can deal cold damage instead.

**Drawback:** You have fire weakness 5.
