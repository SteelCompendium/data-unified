**Benefit:** Your [recovery value](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/rule.health/recoveries) increases by an amount equal to your highest [characteristic](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/rule.character/characteristic) score.

**Drawback:** You have fire weakness 5.
