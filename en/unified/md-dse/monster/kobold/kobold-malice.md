At the start of any kobolds's turn, you can spend [Malice](scc.v1:mcdm.monsters.v1/rule.monster/malice) to activate one of the following features.

> ⭐️ **Maniple Tactics (3 [Malice](scc.v1:mcdm.monsters.v1/rule.monster/malice))**
>
> Up to 3 kobolds make a [free strike](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/feature.common.main-actions/free-strike), swaps positions with an [adjacent](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/rule.combat/adjacent) kobold, and then that kobold makes a [free strike](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/feature.common.main-actions/free-strike).

> 🌀 **Set the Initiative (5 [Malice](scc.v1:mcdm.monsters.v1/rule.monster/malice))**
>
> Two kobolds take their turns in a row.

> ⭐️ **Shield Wall (7 [Malice](scc.v1:mcdm.monsters.v1/rule.monster/malice))**
>
> Until the end of the round, all kobolds with Shield? Shield! impose an additional bane on incoming strikes and abilities.
