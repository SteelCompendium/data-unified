A creature who is [dazed](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/condition/dazed) can do only one thing on their [turn](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/rule.combat/turn): use a main action, use a maneuver, or use a move action. A [dazed](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/condition/dazed) creature also can't use [triggered actions](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/rule.combat/triggered-action), free [triggered actions](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/rule.combat/triggered-action), or [free maneuvers](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/rule.combat/free-maneuver).
