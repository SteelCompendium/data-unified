If you fail a [test](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/rule.test/test) using the Lie skill, you don't suffer any consequences associated with the failure. Additionally, during a negotiation, you can be caught in one lie without negative consequences. When you use either benefit of this perk, you can't use this perk again until you earn 1 or more [Victories](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/rule.resource/victories).
