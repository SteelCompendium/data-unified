During [montage tests](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/rule.test/montage-test), whenever you make a [test](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/rule.test/test) to assist a [test](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/rule.test/test) and obtain a tier 1 outcome, the assisted [test](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/rule.test/test) doesn't take a [bane](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/rule.dice/bane). Additionally, once per [montage test](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/rule.test/montage-test), you can [turn](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/rule.combat/turn) an ally's tier 1 [test](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/rule.test/test) outcome into a tier 2 outcome.
