Whenever a creature watches you sing, dance, or perform a role (as an actor, not just in disguise) for 1 uninterrupted minute or more, you gain an [edge](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/rule.dice/edge) on any [test](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/rule.test/test) made to influence that creature for 1 hour after the performance ends.
