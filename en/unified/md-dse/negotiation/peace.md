An [NPC](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/rule.general/npc) with the [peace](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/negotiation/peace) [motivation](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/rule.negotiation/motivation) wants calm in their life. Under typical circumstances, they want to be left alone to run their business, farm, kingdom, criminal empire, or whatever small slice of the timescape is theirs. Some such [NPCs](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/rule.general/npc) don't have peace and need help obtaining it, while others want their peaceful status quo to be maintained.

An [NPC](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/rule.general/npc) with the [peace](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/negotiation/peace) [pitfall](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/rule.negotiation/pitfall) hates being bored. They want excitement, drama, and danger in their life. For them, there's nothing worse than the status quo.

Arguments that appeal to a peace [motivation](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/rule.negotiation/motivation) contend that helping the heroes will earn the [NPC](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/rule.general/npc) some peace, at least for a little while. Example arguments include the following:

- "You have a good thing going here. A little burgling of nobles, some alcohol smuggling, and some illegal gambling dens. No one's getting hurt, but Constable Cofax is closing in on you. We could redirect him toward some real danger to the community, if you can help us set a trap for the Watchmaker."
- "I know you don't sell to outsiders, but we need that helm. I'm going to use it to [turn](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/rule.combat/turn) back a group of hobgoblins marching this way. They're not going to be as friendly as us."
