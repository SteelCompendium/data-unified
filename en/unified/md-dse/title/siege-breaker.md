*Best way to deal with a castle siege? Be on the outside.*

**Prerequisite:** You have the [Battlefield Commander](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/title/battlefield-commander) title, and you lead the defense of a settlement or fortification.

**Effect:** Choose one of the following benefits:

- *Death From Above:* When you gain an [edge](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/rule.dice/edge) on an ability due to [high ground](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/movement/high-ground), the ability gains a +8 damage [bonus](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/rule.dice/bonuses-and-penalties).
- *Hold the Line:* While you're within 5 squares of an ally, you and each ally within 5 squares of you gains a +3 [bonus](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/rule.dice/bonuses-and-penalties) to [stability](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/rule.character/stability).
- *Last Defender:* Whenever an ally within 5 squares is reduced to 0 [Stamina](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/rule.health/stamina), you gain [temporary Stamina](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/rule.health/temporary-stamina) equal to the ally's level (or 1 if they have no level). If you already have [temporary Stamina](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/rule.health/temporary-stamina) granted by this title, you increase your [temporary Stamina](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/rule.health/temporary-stamina) by the amount you would have gained.
