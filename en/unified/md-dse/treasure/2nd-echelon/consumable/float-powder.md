**[Item Prerequisite](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/rule.downtime/item-prerequisite):** Several strands of hag hair

**[Project Source](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/rule.downtime/project-source):** Texts or lore in Khelt

Additionally, the hag that the powder is sourced from knows exactly where and when you use it.
