*An unseen force seems to draw this steel shield toward nearby creatures.* 

**Keywords:** Psionic, Shield

**[Item Prerequisite](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/rule.downtime/item-prerequisite):** Three ingots of steel, six crystals that resonate with psionic power

**[Project Source](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/rule.downtime/project-source):** Texts or lore in Variac

**[Project Roll](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/rule.downtime/project-roll) [Characteristic](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/rule.character/characteristic):** [Might](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/rule.character/might), [Reason](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/rule.character/reason), or [Intuition](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/rule.character/intuition)

**Project Goal:** 450

**1st Level:** While you wield or carry this shield, you gain a +2 [bonus](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/rule.dice/bonuses-and-penalties) to [Stamina](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/rule.health/stamina). This [Stamina](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/rule.health/stamina) [bonus](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/rule.dice/bonuses-and-penalties) adds to the [Stamina](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/rule.health/stamina) [bonus](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/rule.dice/bonuses-and-penalties) granted by other treasures. Additionally, once per [turn](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/rule.combat/turn) when an [adjacent](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/rule.combat/adjacent) enemy uses an ability, you can use a free [triggered action](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/rule.combat/triggered-action) to use the [Grab](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/feature.common.maneuvers/grab) maneuver against that enemy. You can have any number of enemies [grabbed](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/condition/grabbed) in this way.

**5th Level:** The shield's [bonus](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/rule.dice/bonuses-and-penalties) to [Stamina](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/rule.health/stamina) increases to +5, and you can use the shield's free [triggered action](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/rule.combat/triggered-action) against any enemy within 10 squares who uses an ability. Additionally, any enemy who uses the [Escape Grab](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/feature.common.maneuvers/escape-grab) maneuver while [grabbed](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/condition/grabbed) this way takes a [bane](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/rule.dice/bane) on the [test](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/rule.test/test).

**9th Level:** The shield's [bonus](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/rule.dice/bonuses-and-penalties) to [Stamina](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/rule.health/stamina) increases to +9. Additionally, you can use a maneuver to [pull](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/movement/forced-movement) any number of targets the shield has [grabbed](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/condition/grabbed) up to 5 squares.
