Unless otherwise stated, the following treasures and titles can be wielded by a [summoner](../class/summoner.md) or any hero who comes upon one.

## New Trinkets

The following new [trinkets](../rule/treasure/trinket.md) are available to add to your game.

### 1st-Echelon Trinket

The following 1st-echelon [trinket](../rule/treasure/trinket.md) was designed for [summoners](../class/summoner.md) but can be used by any class.

#### Snakerattle Bangle {data-scc="mcdm.summoner.v1/treasure.1st-echelon.trinket/snakerattle-bangle"}

*This loose-fitting, scaley bangle shifts and slithers while worn. It emits a bone-chilling rattle each time it hits its user's wrist.*

**Keywords:** Arms, Magic

**Item Prerequisite:** The tail of a [size](../rule/character/size.md) 3 or larger rattlesnake

**Project Source:** Texts or lore in Anjali

**Project Roll Characteristic:** [Reason](../rule/character/reason.md) or [Intuition](../rule/character/intuition.md)

**Project Goal:** 150

**Effect:** While wearing this bangle, your melee [free strikes](../feature/common/main-actions/free-strike.md) inflict I < average [frightened](../condition/frightened.md) ([EoT](../rule/combat/end-of-turn.md)). The [potency](../rule/character/potency.md) increases by 1 if you have an [edge](../rule/dice/edge.md) on the [power roll](../rule/dice/power-roll.md).

**Special:** If you are a [summoner](../class/summoner.md), you regain the use of your standard melee [free strike](../feature/common/main-actions/free-strike.md) while wearing this bangle.

### 2nd-Echelon Trinkets

The following 2nd-echelon [trinkets](../rule/treasure/trinket.md) were designed for [summoners](../class/summoner.md) but can be used by any class.

#### Abyssal Map Ink {data-scc="mcdm.summoner.v1/treasure.2nd-echelon.trinket/abyssal-map-ink"}

*The puddle of living ink snarls and snaps while it feeds on parchment and smooth surfaces. It spreads itself across its meal until it resembles a flat, top-down view of its immediate surroundings, wriggling and readjusting itself wherever it goes.*

**Keywords:** Magic

**Item Prerequisite:** A vial of pitling spit, and a jar of human blood

**Project Source:** Texts or lore in Tholl

**Project Roll Characteristic:** [Reason](../rule/character/reason.md) or [Intuition](../rule/character/intuition.md)

**Project Goal:** 300

**Effect:** While the ink is spread on a smooth surface or a piece of parchment paper, it turns into a [size](../rule/character/size.md) 1T or 1S map. This map grants you a double [edge](../rule/dice/edge.md) on [tests](../rule/test/test.md) you make using the [Navigate](../skill/exploration/navigate.md) skill. If you have the map active before the start of an encounter, you can move up to three allies (including yourself ) up to 3 squares away from the starting area.

#### Grasp of the Chained Hand {data-scc="mcdm.summoner.v1/treasure.2nd-echelon.trinket/grasp-of-the-chained-hand"}

*A gauntlet of loose chains rattles, shackled to each of the user's fingers. They clatter whenever magic surges through them.*

**Keywords:** Arms, Magic

**Item Prerequisite:** Blood-soaked chains that restrained a demon for at least 30 days

**Project Source:** Texts or lore in the Tholl

**Project Roll Characteristic:** [Reason](../rule/character/reason.md) or [Presence](../rule/character/presence.md)

**Project Goal:** 300

**Effect:** While wearing this gauntlet, your ranged [free strikes](../feature/common/main-actions/free-strike.md) have the Magic keyword and if the target has P < average, they are magically chained to you until the end of their next turn. While chained, the target can't move farther away from you, and you can use a maneuver to either vertically [pull](../movement/forced-movement.md) the target up to 3 squares or [shift](../movement/shifting.md) yourself up to 3 squares through the air toward the target.

**Special:** If you are a [summoner](../class/summoner.md), you regain the use of your standard ranged [free strike](../feature/common/main-actions/free-strike.md) while wearing this gauntlet.

#### Thunder Chariot {data-scc="mcdm.summoner.v1/treasure.2nd-echelon.trinket/thunder-chariot"}

*A porcelain cart with golden filigree and wheels that crackle with electricity.*

**Keywords:** Magic

**Item Prerequisite:** Twenty lightning bolts frozen in time

**Project Source:** Texts or lore in the First Language

**Project Roll Characteristic:** [Reason](../rule/character/reason.md) or [Intuition](../rule/character/intuition.md)

**Project Goal:** 300

**Effect:** The thunder chariot can be used as a [size](../rule/character/size.md) 2 mount. Its [speed](../rule/character/speed.md) is equal to its rider's [speed](../rule/character/speed.md) + 3.

While riding in the thunder chariot, you have lightning [damage immunity](../rule/damage/damage-immunity.md) 5. An ally can use their maneuver while [adjacent](../rule/combat/adjacent.md) to you to move up to their speed, pulling you along.

### 3rd-Echelon Trinkets

The following 3rd-echelon [trinkets](../rule/treasure/trinket.md) were designed for [summoners](../class/summoner.md) but can be used by any class.

#### Cross of the Scorned Puppeteer {data-scc="mcdm.summoner.v1/treasure.3rd-echelon.trinket/cross-of-the-scorned-puppeteer"}

*This shiny black marionette cross reveals a masked figure standing in its reflection where its wielder should be.*

**Keywords:** Magic, Psionic

**Item Prerequisite:** A spool of silk, a plank of stage wood, and the soul of a famous performer

**Project Source:** A record of a soulbinder ritual in Anjali

**Project Roll Characteristic:** [Reason](../rule/character/reason.md) or [Intuition](../rule/character/intuition.md)

**Project Goal:** 450

**Effect:** While holding the cross, it emits faint strings made of light that you can control with your mind.

Whenever an enemy minion is reduced to 0 [Stamina](../rule/health/stamina.md) within 5 squares of you, you can use a free [triggered action](../rule/combat/triggered-action.md) to attach a string of light to their corpse and command an illusory double of them until the end of the encounter. The minion is organized into the cross's squad of minions with the same name under your command (up to a maximum of six minions). On each of your turns, you can direct the squad to move and use a main action while they're within 5 squares of you.

You can also pull the strings of your allies to keep them out of harm's way. Whenever an ally takes [damage](../rule/damage/damage.md) within 5 squares of you, you can use a [triggered action](../rule/combat/triggered-action.md) to vertically [pull](../movement/forced-movement.md) them 5 squares (ignoring [stability](../rule/character/stability.md)) and halve the [damage](../rule/damage/damage.md).

**Special:** If you are a [summoner](../class/summoner.md) holding the cross, you can organize the minions gained with the cross into one of your class's squads instead.

#### Crystallized Essence {data-scc="mcdm.summoner.v1/treasure.3rd-echelon.trinket/crystallized-essence"}

*A storm rages at the center of this color-shifting shard of rock.*

**Keywords:** Magic

**Item Prerequisite:** The remains of an elemental

**Project Source:** Records in Rhorvic

**Special:** The treasure must be crafted within a cave in Quintessence

**Project Roll Characteristic:** [Reason](../rule/character/reason.md) or [Intuition](../rule/character/intuition.md)

**Project Goal:** 450

**Effect:** While holding crystallized essence, the [distance](../rule/combat/distance.md) of your ranged magic abilities increases by 5. Any creatures you create or summon using magic have their [Stamina](../rule/health/stamina.md) increased by one-third of their Stamina maximum.

Additionally, you can shatter and destroy the crystallized essence as a maneuver to immediately give yourself 5 [essence](../feature/summoner/level-1/essence.md).

#### Warbanner of Pride {data-scc="mcdm.summoner.v1/treasure.3rd-echelon.trinket/warbanner-of-pride"}

*A large banner billows from this tall flagpole. Its heraldry depicts a three-headed lion covered in wounds but still standing.*

**Keywords:** Magic

**Item Prerequisite:** A king's cape and a solid gold flagpole

**Project Source:** Texts or lore in Caelian

**Project Roll Characteristic:** [Reason](../rule/character/reason.md) or [Intuition](../rule/character/intuition.md)

**Project Goal:** 450

**Effect:** While holding the banner, you have a +1 bonus to [saving throws](../rule/general/saving-throw.md) and resisting [potencies](../rule/character/potency.md).

At the end of each of your [turns](../rule/combat/turn.md), each ally within 10 squares of you can make a [saving throw](../rule/general/saving-throw.md) against one effect they are suffering that is ended by a saving throw.

### 4th-Echelon Trinkets

The following 4th-echelon [trinkets](../rule/treasure/trinket.md) were designed for [summoners](../class/summoner.md) but can be used by any class.

#### Hagbasket {data-scc="mcdm.summoner.v1/treasure.4th-echelon.trinket/hagbasket"}

*This throne woven from wood, hair, and metal floats several feet above the ground. The gentle humming it makes while it soars sounds reminiscent of a hag's cackle.*

**Keywords:** Magic

**Item Prerequisite:** A mundane broom, a hag's hut, and a hag's kiss

**Project Source:** Texts or lore in Khelt

**Project Roll Characteristic:** [Reason](../rule/character/reason.md) or [Intuition](../rule/character/intuition.md)

**Project Goal:** 600

**Effect:** The hagbasket can be used as a [size](../rule/character/size.md) 2 mount. Its [speed](../rule/character/speed.md) is equal to its rider's speed, and it can [fly](../movement/fly.md) and [hover](../movement/hover.md).

While riding in the hagbasket, [power rolls](../rule/dice/power-roll.md) made against you have a double [edge](../rule/dice/edge.md). However, you can use a free [triggered action](../rule/combat/triggered-action.md) when a [power roll](../rule/dice/power-roll.md) made against you gets a tier 3 result to halve the [damage](../rule/damage/damage.md).

**Special:** If you are a [summoner](../class/summoner.md) riding in the hagbasket, you can use a maneuver to enable a willing ally within your Summoner's Range to use [Minion Bridge](../feature/ability/summoner/level-1/minion-bridge.md) using your minions.

#### Warbanner of Wrath {data-scc="mcdm.summoner.v1/treasure.4th-echelon.trinket/warbanner-of-wrath"}

*A large banner billows from this tall flagpole. Its heraldry depicts a dragon breathing fire with outstretched wings covered in holes and tears.*

**Keywords:** Magic

**Item Prerequisite:** A king's deathbed veil and a solid brass flagpole

**Project Source:** Texts or lore in Caelian

**Project Roll Characteristic:** [Reason](../rule/character/reason.md) or [Intuition](../rule/character/intuition.md)

**Project Goal:** 600

**Effect:** You can choose to regain 1 [Recovery](../rule/health/recoveries.md) at the end of every combat encounter while holding the banner.

Once per [turn](../rule/combat/turn.md) when you or an ally within 10 squares of you makes a melee [strike](../rule/combat/strike.md), the strike can deal extra [damage](../rule/damage/damage.md) based on the number of [Recoveries](../rule/health/recoveries.md) the striker has left. The strike deals +1 [damage](../rule/damage/damage.md) if the striker has more than half their [Recoveries](../rule/health/recoveries.md) remaining, +2 [damage](../rule/damage/damage.md) if the striker has half or fewer but still at least one of their [Recoveries](../rule/health/recoveries.md) remaining, and +5 [damage](../rule/damage/damage.md) if the striker has no Recoveries.

### Leveled Implement Treasures

The following [implements](../rule/treasure/implement.md) were designed for [summoners](../class/summoner.md) but can be used by any class.

#### 33 Field Commanders Baton {data-scc="mcdm.summoner.v1/treasure.leveled.implement/33-field-commanders-baton"}

*This long, ornate rod with a silver bulb head is braided with 33 green cords. A new cord starts to grow while you wield the baton.*

**Keywords:** Implement, Magic

**Item Prerequisite:** A silver ingot and a written strategy from 33 warleaders

**Project Source:** Texts or lore in Caelian

**Project Roll Characteristic:** [Reason](../rule/character/reason.md), [Intuition](../rule/character/intuition.md), or [Presence](../rule/character/presence.md)

**Project Goal:** 450

**Special:** If you are a [tactician](../class/tactician.md), you can wield this [implement](../rule/treasure/implement.md) as if it had the Light Weapon keyword. Replace any reference to magic or psionic abilities with weapon abilities.

**1st Level:** While you wield this baton, you are imbued with the experience of the commanders who wielded it before you. Your magic or psionic abilities that deal [rolled damage](../rule/damage/rolled-damage.md) gain a +1 [damage](../rule/damage/damage.md) bonus. On your [turn](../rule/combat/turn.md), whenever you or an ally deal [damage](../rule/damage/damage.md) to a creature within 5 squares of you, you can immediately use your maneuver to [slide](../movement/forced-movement.md) the creature up to 2 squares.

**5th Level:** The baton's [damage](../rule/damage/damage.md) bonus increases to +2. When you [slide](../movement/forced-movement.md) a creature using this ability and they end this slide [adjacent](../rule/combat/adjacent.md) to one of your allies, that ally can use the [Grab](../feature/common/maneuvers/grab.md) or [Knockback](../feature/common/maneuvers/knockback.md) maneuver against the creature as a free [triggered action](../rule/combat/triggered-action.md).

**9th Level:** The baton's [damage](../rule/damage/damage.md) bonus increases to +3. On your [turn](../rule/combat/turn.md), whenever you or an ally deal [damage](../rule/damage/damage.md) to a creature within 10 squares of you, you can immediately use your maneuver to [slide](../movement/forced-movement.md) the creature up to 3 squares, ignoring [stability](../rule/character/stability.md).

#### Rex Scepter {data-scc="mcdm.summoner.v1/treasure.leveled.implement/rex-scepter"}

*The rod resembles a simple tree branch. It grows and braids itself into an ornate scepter in the heat of battle.*

**Keywords:** Implement, Magic

**Item Prerequisite:** A tree branch and fealty from three hundred or more sworn followers

**Project Source:** Texts or lore in Zaliac

**Project Goal:** 450

**1st Level:** While you wield this scepter, your [Renown](../rule/resource/renown.md) is increased by 1. Additionally, you can use a main action once per [turn](../rule/combat/turn.md) targeting a willing ally or two minions you control within 5 squares of you to make a [free strike](../feature/common/main-actions/free-strike.md) on a target within the same [distance](../rule/combat/distance.md) of you as a free [triggered action](../rule/combat/triggered-action.md).

**5th Level:** The scepter's bonus to [Renown](../rule/resource/renown.md) increases to +2. Additionally, whenever a creature you command using the scepter makes a [free strike](../feature/common/main-actions/free-strike.md), they gain 1 [surge](../rule/resource/surge.md) that can be used immediately.

**9th Level:** The scepter's bonus to [Renown](../rule/resource/renown.md) increases to +4. Additionally, the scepter's main action can now be used as a maneuver.

#### Sanctuary Horn {data-scc="mcdm.summoner.v1/treasure.leveled.implement/sanctuary-horn"}

*This spiral hunting horn is embellished with branching veins of copper across the body. The metal glows red hot as the horn is blown.*

**Keywords:** Implement, Magic

**Item Prerequisite:** The spiral shell of a fallen armory snail and whirlwinds captured from Quintessence

**Project Source:** Texts or lore in Kalliak

**Project Goal:** 450

**1st Level:** While you wield the horn, your magic and psionic [strikes](../rule/combat/strike.md) deal sonic [damage](../rule/damage/damage.md) instead of their usual damage. Additionally, you can use a maneuver once per [turn](../rule/combat/turn.md) to blow the horn, allowing one ally or up to two minions you control within 5 squares of you to be recalled, instantly [teleporting](../movement/teleport.md) them into unoccupied spaces [adjacent](../rule/combat/adjacent.md) to you.

**5th Level:** While you wield the horn, the area of your [cube](../rule/combat/cube.md), [burst](../rule/combat/burst.md), and [aura](../rule/combat/aura.md) magic or psionic abilities increases by 1. Additionally, whenever a creature is [teleported](../movement/teleport.md) by this horn, they can choose to either spend a [Recovery](../rule/health/recoveries.md) or gain a [surge](../rule/resource/surge.md).

**9th Level:** The horn's area bonus increases to 2. After you blow the horn as a maneuver, you can [teleport](../movement/teleport.md) yourself, another ally, or up to two other minions you control within 5 squares of you into the space left behind by a recalled target, provided they fit into the space.

#### Wand of the Unheard Orchestra {data-scc="mcdm.summoner.v1/treasure.leveled.implement/wand-of-the-unheard-orchestra"}

*This conductor's baton has an unassuming and inornate steel body. It increases in length when it's swung and flashes a bright light when wanded at a regular interval.*

## New Titles

The following titles are available to add to your game.

### 1st-Echelon Title

The following title was designed for [summoners](../class/summoner.md) but is available for any hero.

#### Safeguarded {data-scc="mcdm.summoner.v1/title/safeguarded"}

*They risk their lives for me because I risk my life for theirs. This fight belongs to all of us!*

**Prerequisite:** You earn a noble rank or earn the favor of a knight's guild.

**Effect:** You start combat encounters with a squad of three minions from the specific monster band or people you earned this title from. The squad takes their [turn](../rule/combat/turn.md) as a part of your turn. On the squad's turn, they can take a move action and either a main action or a maneuver.

**Special:** If two or more heroes in the party have this title from the same source, the party starts combat encounters with a squad of six minions instead. At the start of each [round](../rule/combat/combat-round.md) of combat, the heroes decide who controls the squad as a part of their turn.

### 2nd-Echelon Titles

The following titles were designed for [summoners](../class/summoner.md) but are available for any hero.

#### Sigilwright {data-scc="mcdm.summoner.v1/title/sigilwright"}

*Let's find out who's on the other side of the circle.*

**Prerequisite:** You complete a ritual to summon a non-minion entity using a summoning circle you drew.

**Effect:** Choose one of the following benefits:

*Quick Gate:* As a maneuver, you can etch an arcane circle in an [adjacent](../rule/combat/adjacent.md) square on the ground. Until the end of the encounter, you can use magic abilities and treat your Summoner's Range as if you were in that space. Each time you etch an arcane circle, your last circle fades and becomes inactive.

*Ritual Circle:* As a [respite](../rule/resource/respite.md) activity, you can draw a minor summoning circle to temporarily call forth a creature you've contacted previously, regardless of manifold. If the creature is willing and able to answer your call, they appear to you for the remainder of the [respite](../rule/resource/respite.md). You can ask the creature for information or a service in exchange for ending the [respite](../rule/resource/respite.md) with two fewer [Recoveries](../rule/health/recoveries.md) or a demand of their choosing. If the creature doesn't appear, you can choose to take a different [respite](../rule/resource/respite.md) activity.

*Sigil Eye:* You have an [edge](../rule/dice/edge.md) on [tests](../rule/test/test.md) made to identify summoning circles and who or where they're connected to. You also have an [edge](../rule/dice/edge.md) on [strikes](../rule/combat/strike.md) made against creatures not native to the manifold in which you're currently located.

#### Summoner Successor {data-scc="mcdm.summoner.v1/title/summoner-successor"}

*When their leader fell, they started listening to me for some reason.*

**Prerequisite:** You can't use the [Call Forth](../feature/ability/summoner/level-1/call-forth.md) ability, and you defeat a leader or solo creature with a Summon or Call Forth ability, such as a [high elf](../ancestry/high-elf.md) ordinator.

**Effect:** Choose a signature minion from a [summoner's](../class/summoner.md) [portfolio](../feature/summoner/level-1/portfolio.md) that shares a keyword with one of the creatures the summoner could summon. You can summon three of that minion into a single squad (up to a maximum of four creatures) at the start of each of your [turns](../rule/combat/turn.md) in combat. The [distance](../rule/combat/distance.md) you can command them is equal to your ranged [free strike](../feature/common/main-actions/free-strike.md) [distance](../rule/combat/distance.md). You also gain the [Strike for Me](../feature/ability/summoner/level-1/strike-for-me.md) [triggered action](../rule/combat/triggered-action.md), which now has the Psionic keyword and loses the Magic keyword.

Additionally, you can summon up to two of your [signature minion](../feature/summoner/level-1/minions.md) while outside of combat to do simple tasks.

### 3rd-Echelon Title

The following title was designed for [summoners](../class/summoner.md) but is available for any hero.

#### Ringleader {data-scc="mcdm.summoner.v1/title/ringleader"}

*Don't worry. I've got a guy.*

**Prerequisite:** You complete three downtime projects during the same [respite](../rule/resource/respite.md) with the help of [followers](../rule/general/follower.md) or minions.

**Effect:** You no longer need [line of effect](../rule/combat/line-of-effect.md) to give commands to any minions you can summon. Additionally, choose one of the following benefits:

*For the Boss:* Your [followers](../rule/general/follower.md) gain a +3 bonus to [project rolls](../rule/downtime/project-roll.md) that they make.

*Networker:* Your maximum [follower](../rule/general/follower.md) count increases by 1.

*Stringpuller:* While occupying a civilized area (such as a village, town, district, or city), you always have access to a follower native to the location. If the area is otherwise hostile to you, this follower is a spy in hiding who has any project points they earn halved.

### 4th-Echelon Titles

The following titles are available for [summoners](../class/summoner.md).

#### Delegator {data-scc="mcdm.summoner.v1/title/delegator"}

*They're all yours, buddy.*

**Prerequisite:** You are a [summoner](../class/summoner.md), and you strike a deal with your [portfolio's](../feature/summoner/level-1/portfolio.md) champion.

**Effect:** At the start of a combat encounter, you can choose to translate yourself into your circle's source manifold while your champion fights and summons monsters in your place. The champion uses your [Stamina](../rule/health/stamina.md), [Recoveries](../rule/health/recoveries.md), abilities, and features (except for your [Summoner Strike](../feature/summoner/level-1/summoner-strike.md) and [Summoner's Kit](../feature/summoner/level-3/summoners-kit.md)). You can dismiss the champion and summon yourself back into the place you left at the end of an encounter.

If your champion would die while taking your place, you lose access to your [Return to the Source](../feature/summoner/level-6/return-to-the-source.md) feature and can only summon signature minions until you revive your champion as a [respite](../rule/resource/respite.md) activity.

#### High Summoner of the Circle {data-scc="mcdm.summoner.v1/title/high-summoner-of-the-circle"}

*As I was taught, so I pass on to you.*

**Prerequisite:** You are a [summoner](../class/summoner.md), and you teach someone how to call forth two or more minions from your [portfolio](../feature/summoner/level-1/portfolio.md).

**Effect:** You are considered a master that can be learned from using the [Learn from a Master](../project/learn-from-a-master.md) downtime project in Draw Steel: Heroes. Anyone that makes a [project roll](../rule/downtime/project-roll.md) using you as the source gains a bonus to their roll equal to your [Reason](../rule/character/reason.md). Additionally, choose one of the following benefits:

*Essence Mastery:* Your minions cost one fewer [essence](../feature/summoner/level-1/essence.md) to summon (minimum cost of 1 essence).

*Expanded Domain:* Your Summoner's Range increases by 5. You can use your minions' senses as your own as long as they're on the same manifold as you.

*Signature Summoner:* Select a 3-[Essence](../feature/summoner/level-1/essence.md) minion you can [Call Forth](../feature/ability/summoner/level-1/call-forth.md). At the start of each of your [turns](../rule/combat/turn.md) in combat, you summon the set number of minions listed on the stat block in place of your signature minions at no cost.
