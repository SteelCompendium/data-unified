You have an epic resource called [virtue](virtue.md). Each time you finish a [respite](../../../rule/resource/respite.md), you gain [virtue](virtue.md) equal to the [XP](../../../rule/resource/experience.md) you gain. You can spend [virtue](virtue.md) on your abilities as if it were wrath.

Additionally, you can spend 3 [virtue](virtue.md) to access one of your deity's domains that you usually don't have access to. When you do, you can use that domain's features until you finish another [respite](../../../rule/resource/respite.md).

[Virtue](virtue.md) remains until you spend it.
