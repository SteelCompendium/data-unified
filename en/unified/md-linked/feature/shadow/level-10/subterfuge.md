You have an epic resource called [subterfuge](subterfuge.md). Each time you finish a [respite](../../../rule/resource/respite.md), you gain [subterfuge](subterfuge.md) equal to the [XP](../../../rule/resource/experience.md) you gain. You can spend [subterfuge](subterfuge.md) on your abilities as if it were insight.

Additionally, you can spend [subterfuge](subterfuge.md) to take additional maneuvers on your [turn](../../../rule/combat/turn.md). You can use one maneuver for each [subterfuge](subterfuge.md) you spend.

[Subterfuge](subterfuge.md) remains until you spend it.
