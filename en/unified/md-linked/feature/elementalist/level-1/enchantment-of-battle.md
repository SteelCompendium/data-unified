You can wear light armor and wield light weapons effectively, even though you don't have a kit. While you wear light armor, you gain a +3 [bonus](../../../rule/dice/bonuses-and-penalties.md) to [Stamina](../../../rule/health/stamina.md), and that [bonus](../../../rule/dice/bonuses-and-penalties.md) increases by 3 at 4th, 7th, and 10th levels. While you wield a light weapon, you gain a +1 damage [bonus](../../../rule/dice/bonuses-and-penalties.md) with weapon abilities, including [free strikes](../../common/main-actions/free-strike.md). You can use light armor treasures and light weapon treasures.

If you have a kit, you can't take this [enchantment](enchantment.md).
