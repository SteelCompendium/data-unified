When a creature takes the [Defend](defend.md) main action, [ability rolls](../../../rule/dice/ability-roll.md) made against them have a double [bane](../../../rule/dice/bane.md) until the start of their next [turn](../../../rule/combat/turn.md). Additionally, you have a double [edge](../../../rule/dice/edge.md) on [tests](../../../rule/test/test.md) when called for to resist environmental effects or a creature's traits or abilities. A creature gains no benefit from this action while another creature is [taunted](../../../condition/taunted.md) by them (see [Conditions](../../../rule/combat/condition.md) in Chapter 5: [Classes](../../../chapter/classes.md)).
