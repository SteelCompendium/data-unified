A creature who uses the [Heal](heal.md) main action employs medicine or inspiring words to make an [adjacent](../../../rule/combat/adjacent.md) creature feel better and stay in the fight. The target creature can spend a [Recovery](../../../rule/health/recoveries.md) to regain [Stamina](../../../rule/health/stamina.md), or can make a [saving throw](../../../rule/general/saving-throw.md) against one effect they are suffering that is ended by a [saving throw](../../../rule/general/saving-throw.md).
