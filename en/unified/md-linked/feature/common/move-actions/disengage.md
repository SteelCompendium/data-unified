When a creature takes the [Disengage](disengage.md) move action, they can [shift](../../../movement/shifting.md) 1 square. Certain class features, kits, and other rules allow a creature to [shift](../../../movement/shifting.md) more than 1 square when they disengage. A creature who does so can break up their [shift](../../../movement/shifting.md) with their maneuver and main action however they wish.
