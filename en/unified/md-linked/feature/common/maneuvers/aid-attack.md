type: feature
---

A creature who uses the [Aid Attack](aid-attack.md) maneuver chooses an enemy [adjacent](../../../rule/combat/adjacent.md) to them. The next [ability roll](../../../rule/dice/ability-roll.md) an ally makes against that enemy before the start of the aiding creature's next [turn](../../../rule/combat/turn.md) gains an [edge](../../../rule/dice/edge.md).
