Unless otherwise noted in its description, a creature can activate a [consumable](../../../rule/treasure/consumable.md) treasure such as a potion with the [Use Consumable](use-consumable.md) maneuver. A creature can use this maneuver to administer a [consumable](../../../rule/treasure/consumable.md) treasure that benefits the user either to themself or to a willing [adjacent](../../../rule/combat/adjacent.md) creature. See [Consumables](../../../rule/treasure/consumable.md) in Chapter 13: [Rewards](../../../chapter/rewards.md).
