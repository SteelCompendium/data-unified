Using the [Hide](hide.md) maneuver, a creature attempts to hide from other creatures who aren't observing them while they have [cover](../../../rule/combat/cover.md) or [concealment](../../../rule/combat/concealment.md). See Hide and Sneak in Chapter 9: [Tests](../../../chapter/tests.md) for full details.
