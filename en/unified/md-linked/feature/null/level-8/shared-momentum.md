When you take the [Disengage](../../common/move-actions/disengage.md) move action, one ally in the area of your [Null Field](../level-1/null-field.md) ability can also take the [Disengage](../../common/move-actions/disengage.md) move action as a free [triggered action](../../../rule/combat/triggered-action.md), using your [distance](../../../rule/combat/distance.md) for that move action.
