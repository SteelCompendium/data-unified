While using your [Null Field](../level-1/null-field.md) ability, you disrupt magic and psionic power suffusing the area around you. During combat, any temporary [supernatural](../../../rule/general/supernatural.md) terrain effects of your level or lower are removed when your [aura](../../../rule/combat/aura.md) partially or fully overlaps with their location. Permanent [supernatural](../../../rule/general/supernatural.md) terrain effects of your level or lower are temporarily negated while your [aura](../../../rule/combat/aura.md) overlaps with their location, but return when the [aura](../../../rule/combat/aura.md) no longer overlaps with them.
