You know the Mindspeech language (see Languages in [Orden](../../../rule/world/orden.md) in Chapter 4: [Background](../../../chapter/background.md)). Additionally, you can telepathically communicate with any creatures within [distance](../../../rule/combat/distance.md) of your [Mind Spike](../../ability/talent/level-1/mind-spike.md) ability if they share a language with you and you know of each other. When you communicate with someone this way, they can respond telepathically.
