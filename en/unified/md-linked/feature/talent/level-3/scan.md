You can extend your psionic senses beyond their usual range. Once on each of your [turns](../../../rule/combat/turn.md), you can search for hidden creatures as a [free maneuver](../../../rule/combat/free-maneuver.md) (see Hide and Sneak in Chapter 9: [Tests](../../../chapter/tests.md)). Additionally, once you establish [line of effect](../../../rule/combat/line-of-effect.md) to a thinking creature within [distance](../../../rule/combat/distance.md) of your [Mind Spike](../../ability/talent/level-1/mind-spike.md) ability, you always have [line of effect](../../../rule/combat/line-of-effect.md) to that creature until they move beyond that [distance](../../../rule/combat/distance.md).
