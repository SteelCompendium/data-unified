You gain 1 additional [drama](../level-1/drama.md) or other [Heroic Resource](../../../rule/resource/heroic-resource.md) whenever you use your [Appeal to the Muses](../level-2/appeal-to-the-muses.md) feature. Additionally, your performances no longer have a [distance](../../../rule/combat/distance.md), but can affect any target on the encounter map within your [line of effect](../../../rule/combat/line-of-effect.md).
