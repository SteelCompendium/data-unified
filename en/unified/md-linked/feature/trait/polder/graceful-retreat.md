Your small [size](../../../rule/character/size.md) makes it easier for you to slip away from the fray. You gain a +1 [bonus](../../../rule/dice/bonuses-and-penalties.md) to the [distance](../../../rule/combat/distance.md) you can shift when you take the [Disengage](../../common/move-actions/disengage.md) move action.
