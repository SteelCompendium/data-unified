You create a 1-[aura](../../../rule/combat/aura.md) vortex of slicing magic around you when you enter combat. An enemy that starts their [turn](../../../rule/combat/turn.md) [adjacent](../../../rule/combat/adjacent.md) to you takes [damage](../../../rule/damage/damage.md) equal to your [Reason](../../../rule/character/reason.md).
