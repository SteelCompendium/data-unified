Your maximum number of [followers](../../../rule/general/follower.md) increases by 2.

You can summon and recruit an artisan [follower](../../../rule/general/follower.md) and a sage [follower](../../../rule/general/follower.md) that share a keyword with a minion you can summon. These followers can be creatures from your portfolio or preexisting denizens of your circle's source manifold. See Follower Types under Attract Followers in Draw Steel: Heroes for information on constructing your followers' stats.
