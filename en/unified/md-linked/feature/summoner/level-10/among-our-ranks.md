As a [respite](../../../rule/resource/respite.md) activity, you summon a willing and not-[restrained](../../../condition/restrained.md) [NPC](../../../rule/general/npc.md) or player ally to join your party, regardless of [distance](../../../rule/combat/distance.md) or manifold. The target stays until the start of your next [respite](../../../rule/resource/respite.md) or until they are killed, in which they are immediately dismissed to the place from which they were summoned. You can't have more than one character summoned in this way.
