While you have [line of effect](../../../rule/combat/line-of-effect.md) to a creature with a soul, you can perceive a trail of where the creature has been in the last number of minutes equal to 5 × your level.

When you finish a [respite](../../../rule/resource/respite.md), you can always perceive the soul trails of each creature that took the [respite](../../../rule/resource/respite.md) with you until your next [respite](../../../rule/resource/respite.md).
