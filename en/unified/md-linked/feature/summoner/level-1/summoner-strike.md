You have the following ability, which replaces both your melee and ranged [free strikes](../../common/main-actions/free-strike.md).

## Summoner Strike {data-scc="mcdm.summoner.v1/feature.ability.summoner.level-1/summoner-strike"}

*A sudden burst of energy erupts from your implement and shocks your foes' nerves.*

| **Magic, Melee, Ranged, Strike** |          **Main action** |
|----------------------------------|--------------------------:|
| **📏 Melee 1 or Ranged 5**      | **🎯 One creature or object** |

**Effect:** R [damage](../../../rule/damage/damage.md). If the target has R < WEAK, they are [slowed](../../../condition/slowed.md) (save ends).

**Special:** This ability has the Charge keyword when it's used as a melee [strike](../../../rule/combat/strike.md).
