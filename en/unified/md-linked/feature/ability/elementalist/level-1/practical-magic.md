*Your mastery of elemental power lets you customize your conjurations.*

| **Magic**              | **[Maneuver](../../../../rule/combat/turn.md)** |
|------------------------|-------------:|
| **📏 Self; see below** |  **🎯 Self** |

**Effect:** Choose one of the following effects:

- You use the [Knockback](../../../common/maneuvers/knockback.md) maneuver (see Chapter 10: [Combat](../../../../chapter/combat.md)), but its [distance](../../../../rule/combat/distance.md) becomes the range of your [Hurl Element](hurl-element.md) ability, and you use [Reason](../../../../rule/character/reason.md) instead of [Might](../../../../rule/character/might.md) for the [power roll](../../../../rule/dice/power-roll.md).
- You choose a creature within the [distance](../../../../rule/combat/distance.md) of your [Hurl Element](hurl-element.md) ability and one of the following [damage types](../../../../rule/damage/damage-type.md): acid, cold, corruption, fire, lightning, poison, or sonic. That creature takes damage of the chosen type equal to your [Reason](../../../../rule/character/reason.md) score.
- You [teleport](../../../../movement/teleport.md) up to a number of squares equal to your [Reason](../../../../rule/character/reason.md) score. If you choose this option, you can spend essence to [teleport](../../../../movement/teleport.md) 1 additional square for each essence spent.
