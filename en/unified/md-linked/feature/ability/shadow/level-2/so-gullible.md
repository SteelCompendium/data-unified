*When your enemy strikes, you reveal you were in a different place all along.*

| **Magic**            | **Free [triggered](../../../../rule/combat/triggered-action.md)** |
|----------------------|-------------------:|
| **📏 Self**          |        **🎯 Self** |

**Trigger:** Another creature targets you with a [strike](../../../../rule/combat/strike.md).

**Effect:** You use your [Clever Trick](../level-1/clever-trick.md) ability with no insight cost against the triggering creature and strike. You can [teleport](../../../../movement/teleport.md) to an unoccupied space within 3 squares of that creature and can make a [free strike](../../../common/main-actions/free-strike.md) against them. You can then spend a [Recovery](../../../../rule/health/recoveries.md).
