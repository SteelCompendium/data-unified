If you spend 10 minutes or less interacting with a creature who hasn't met you before, you can cause them to forget your face when you part. If asked to describe you, the creature gives only a vague, blank, and unhelpful description. Additionally, if you spend 1 hour or more assembling a disguise, you automatically obtain a tier 2 outcome on any [test](../rule/test/test.md) that could make use of the Disguise skill. If you have the Disguise skill, you automatically obtain a tier 3 outcome on the [test](../rule/test/test.md).
