As a child, you met a creature who [turns](../rule/combat/turn.md) people to stone, such as a medusa. You escaped half petrified, avoiding the fate of others who stand as statues now.

**Benefit:** You have a +1 [bonus](../rule/dice/bonuses-and-penalties.md) to [stability](../rule/character/stability.md). Additionally, you can use a [free maneuver](../rule/combat/free-maneuver.md) to cause your body, gear, and any items you hold to take on the appearance of stone, making you appear to be a [mundane](mundane.md) statue while you remain unmoving.

**Drawback:** You have sonic weakness 5. Additionally, while you are [winded](../rule/health/winded.md), you are [dazed](../condition/dazed.md).
