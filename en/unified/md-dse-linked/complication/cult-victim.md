Cultists captured you while raiding your home, then began an unholy ritual to [turn](../rule/combat/turn.md) your body into an undead spirit. Though the ritual failed, your body became infused with corrupted magic, turning you partially incorporeal.

**Benefit:** Once per [turn](../rule/combat/turn.md), you can move through solid matter 1 square thick or less. If you end your [turn](../rule/combat/turn.md) inside solid matter, you are forced out into the space from which you entered it and you take 5 damage that can't be reduced in any way.

**Drawback:** You have corruption weakness 5.
