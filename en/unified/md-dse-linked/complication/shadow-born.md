You were born in the dusk land ruled by the Queen of [Shadows](../class/shadow.md), and its darkness has seeped into your bones. (See The Myriad Worlds of the Timescape in Chapter 1: [The Basics](../chapter/the-basics.md).)

**Benefit:** Whenever you start your [turn](../rule/combat/turn.md) with [concealment](../rule/combat/concealment.md), you gain 1 [surge](../rule/resource/surge.md).

**Drawback:** You have holy weakness 5.
