A creature who is [dazed](dazed.md) can do only one thing on their [turn](../rule/combat/turn.md): use a main action, use a maneuver, or use a move action. A [dazed](dazed.md) creature also can't use [triggered actions](../rule/combat/triggered-action.md), free [triggered actions](../rule/combat/triggered-action.md), or [free maneuvers](../rule/combat/free-maneuver.md).
