At the start of any level 4 or higher demon's turn, you can spend [Malice](../../../rule/monster/malice.md) to activate one of the following features.

> ⭐️ **Prior Malice Features (3-7 [Malice](../../../rule/monster/malice.md))**
>
> The demon activates a [Malice](../../../rule/monster/malice.md) feature available to demons of level or lower.

> 👤 **Abyssal Jaunt (3 [Malice](../../../rule/monster/malice.md))**
>
> One demon acting this turn folds space around them, temporarily slipping in and out of the Abyssal Wasteland. Until the start of the demon's next turn, they ignore [difficult terrain](../../../movement/difficult-terrain.md), can move through solid matter, and don't provoke [opportunity attacks](../../../rule/combat/opportunity-attack.md) by moving. If the demon ends their turn inside solid matter, they are shunted out into the space from which they entered it.
