Defenders are tough creatures able to take a lot of damage, and who can force enemies to attack them instead of squishier targets. Defenders often act in [squads](../monster/squad.md) with allies who have lower [Stamina](../health/stamina.md), such as [controllers](controller.md) and [hexers](hexer.md).
