A [nomadic](nomadic.md) culture travels from place to place to survive. Members of a [nomadic](nomadic.md) culture might follow animal migrations or the weather, travel to sell their wares or services, or simply enjoy a restless lifestyle full of new experiences and peoples. Those who grow up in [nomadic](nomadic.md) cultures learn to navigate the wilderness and work closely with others to survive.

**Skill Options:** One skill from the [exploration](../skill/group/exploration.md) or [interpersonal](../skill/group/interpersonal.md) skill groups. (*Quick Build:* [Navigate](../skill/exploration/navigate.md).)
