**Power Roll + Reason:**

- **≤11:** 5 [damage](../../../../rule/damage/damage.md); [push](../../../../movement/forced-movement.md) 2
- **12-16:** 9 [damage](../../../../rule/damage/damage.md); [push](../../../../movement/forced-movement.md) 4
- **17+:** 12 [damage](../../../../rule/damage/damage.md); [push](../../../../movement/forced-movement.md) 6

Each target takes an additional 1 [damage](../../../../rule/damage/damage.md), plus 1 [damage](../../../../rule/damage/damage.md) for each [minion](../../../summoner/level-1/minions.md) killed this way. These [minions](../../../summoner/level-1/minions.md) activate no effects upon death, and you gain no [essence](../../../summoner/level-1/essence.md) from their deaths.
