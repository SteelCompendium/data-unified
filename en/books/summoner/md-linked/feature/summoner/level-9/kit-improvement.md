You've conjured your kit into its ultimate form. You have the following benefits:

- The [potency](../../../rule/character/potency.md) of your [Summoner Strike](../../ability/summoner/level-1/summoner-strike.md) ability increases to R < STRONG.
- You can choose one additional ward from your [Summoner's Kit](../level-3/summoners-kit.md).
- You have a double [edge](../../../rule/dice/edge.md) on [tests](../../../rule/test/test.md) made to dissuade or scare enemy minions or lackeys.
- Your clothing and equipment become adorned with distinct and elaborate regalia to make you stand out from your army, like massive rib cage pauldrons, a tooth crested helmet, or a billowing mantle of fire.
