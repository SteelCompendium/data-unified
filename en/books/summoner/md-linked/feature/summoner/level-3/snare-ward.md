Whenever an [adjacent](../../../rule/combat/adjacent.md) creature deals [damage](../../../rule/damage/damage.md) to you, you can use a [free triggered action](../../../rule/combat/triggered-action.md) to pull that creature toward one of your [minions](../level-1/minions.md) within your Summoner's Range a number of squares equal to your [Reason](../../../rule/character/reason.md) score.
