You are clad in the natural defenses of your [portfolio](../level-1/portfolio.md) (bones, fairy wood, stone, writhing flesh). You gain a +3 bonus to [Stamina](../../../rule/health/stamina.md) and that bonus increases by 3 at 4th, 7th, and 10th levels.
