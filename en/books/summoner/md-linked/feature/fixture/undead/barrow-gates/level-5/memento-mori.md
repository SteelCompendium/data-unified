You gain a [surge](../../../../../rule/resource/surge.md) the first time in a [round](../../../../../rule/combat/combat-round.md) one of your undead [minions](../../../../summoner/level-1/minions.md) unwillingly dies while you have [line of effect](../../../../../rule/combat/line-of-effect.md) to the gates. You can choose to give the [surge](../../../../../rule/resource/surge.md) to an ally who also has [line of effect](../../../../../rule/combat/line-of-effect.md) to the gates.
