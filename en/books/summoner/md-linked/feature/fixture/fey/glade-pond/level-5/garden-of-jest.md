You can spend a [Recovery](../../../../../rule/health/recoveries.md) the first time in a [round](../../../../../rule/combat/combat-round.md) a creature gains or starts their [turn](../../../../../rule/combat/turn.md) with a [condition](../../../../../rule/combat/condition.md) while you have [line of effect](../../../../../rule/combat/line-of-effect.md) to the pond. Alternatively, you can choose to enable an ally who also has [line of effect](../../../../../rule/combat/line-of-effect.md) to the boil to spend a [Recovery](../../../../../rule/health/recoveries.md) instead.
