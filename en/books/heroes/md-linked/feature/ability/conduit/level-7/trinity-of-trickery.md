*Hey! I'm over here. No, here, numbskull.*

| **Magic, [Ranged](../../../../rule/combat/ranged.md)**  |            **Maneuver** |
|--------------------|------------------------:|
| **📏 [Ranged](../../../../rule/combat/ranged.md) 10**   | **🎯 Self or one ally** |

**Effect:** You create two illusory duplicates of the target, which appear anywhere within [distance](../../../../rule/combat/distance.md). These duplicates last until the end of the encounter. On each of their [turns](../../../../rule/combat/turn.md), the target can move each duplicate up to their [speed](../../../../rule/character/speed.md). If the target is targeted by an ability, they can use a free [triggered action](../../../conduit/level-1/triggered-action.md) to switch places with a duplicate within their [line of effect](../../../../rule/combat/line-of-effect.md), making the duplicate the target of the ability instead. When either duplicate takes damage, it is destroyed.
