*In your anger, you revert to a more bestial form.*

| **-**       | **[Triggered](../../../../rule/combat/triggered-action.md)** |
|-------------|--------------:|
| **📏 Self** |   **🎯 Self** |

**Trigger:** You lose [Stamina](../../../../rule/health/stamina.md) and are not [dying](../../../../rule/health/dying.md).
