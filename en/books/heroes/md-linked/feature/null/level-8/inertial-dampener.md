You and each creature or object of your choice in the area of your [Null Field](../level-1/null-field.md) ability gain a [bonus](../../../rule/dice/bonuses-and-penalties.md) to [stability](../../../rule/character/stability.md) equal to your [Intuition](../../../rule/character/intuition.md) score. A creature who attempts to [force move](../../../movement/forced-movement.md) a target with this [bonus](../../../rule/dice/bonuses-and-penalties.md) takes psychic damage equal to your [Intuition](../../../rule/character/intuition.md) score.
