The flow of psionic power through you allows you to achieve high velocity. You gain a [bonus](../../../rule/dice/bonuses-and-penalties.md) to [speed](../../../rule/character/speed.md) and to the number of squares you can [shift](../../../movement/shifting.md) when you take the [Disengage](../../common/move-actions/disengage.md) move action equal to your [Agility](../../../rule/character/agility.md) score.
