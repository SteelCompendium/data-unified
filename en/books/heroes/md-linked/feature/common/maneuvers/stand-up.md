A creature can use the [Stand Up](stand-up.md) maneuver to stand up if they [are prone](../../../condition/prone.md), ending that [condition](../../../rule/combat/condition.md). Alternatively, they can use this maneuver to make a willing [adjacent](../../../rule/combat/adjacent.md) prone creature stand up.
