You gain an [edge](../../../rule/dice/edge.md) on [tests](../../../rule/test/test.md) made to hide and sneak. Additionally, whenever you fall, you can use a free [triggered action](../../../rule/combat/triggered-action.md) to use your [Aspect of the Wild](../../ability/fury/stormwight-kits/aspect-of-the-wild.md) ability.
