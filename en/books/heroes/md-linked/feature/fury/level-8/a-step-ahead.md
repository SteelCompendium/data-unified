You move with legendary grace. Whenever you make an [Agility](../../../rule/character/agility.md) [test](../../../rule/test/test.md), you can roll three dice and choose which two to use. Additionally, whenever you use the [Disengage](../../common/move-actions/disengage.md) move action, the [distance](../../../rule/combat/distance.md) you can [shift](../../../movement/shifting.md) gains a [bonus](../../../rule/dice/bonuses-and-penalties.md) equal to your [Agility](../../../rule/character/agility.md) score.
