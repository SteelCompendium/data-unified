Whenever a creature deals damage to you that leaves you [dying](../../../rule/health/dying.md), you can make a [free strike](../../common/main-actions/free-strike.md) against any creature. If the creature is reduced to 0 [Stamina](../../../rule/health/stamina.md) by your [strike](../../../rule/combat/strike.md), you can spend a [Recovery](../../../rule/health/recoveries.md).
