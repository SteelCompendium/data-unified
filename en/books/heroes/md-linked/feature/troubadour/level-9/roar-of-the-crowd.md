You are empowered by your audience, near and far. You can't be made [frightened](../../../condition/frightened.md), and if you [are prone](../../../condition/prone.md), you can stand up as a [free maneuver](../../../rule/combat/free-maneuver.md).

Additionally, whenever you spend a [Recovery](../../../rule/health/recoveries.md), you can forgo regaining [Stamina](../../../rule/health/stamina.md) to invoke the roar of an invisible applauding audience. You and each ally within 3 squares of you gains [temporary Stamina](../../../rule/health/temporary-stamina.md) equal to 10 + the number of active bonds from your [Scene Partner](../level-1/scene-partner.md) feature + either your [Victories](../../../rule/resource/victories.md) or the number of players in your game (whichever is higher).
