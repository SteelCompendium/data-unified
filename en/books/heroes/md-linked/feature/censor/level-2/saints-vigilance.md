You have honed your ability to detect sin and can use it to find those who hide from justice. Any creature judged by you can't use the [Hide](../../common/maneuvers/hide.md) maneuver. Additionally, you gain an [edge](../../../rule/dice/edge.md) when searching for hidden creatures. If you find a hidden creature, you can use your [Judgment](../../ability/censor/level-1/judgment.md) ability against them as a free [triggered action](../../../rule/combat/triggered-action.md).
