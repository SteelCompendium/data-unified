Your god blesses your flesh and infuses it with divine quickness. You gain a +1 [bonus](../../../rule/dice/bonuses-and-penalties.md) to [speed](../../../rule/character/speed.md) and to the [distance](../../../rule/combat/distance.md) you can [shift](../../../movement/shifting.md) when you take the [Disengage](../../common/move-actions/disengage.md) move action.
