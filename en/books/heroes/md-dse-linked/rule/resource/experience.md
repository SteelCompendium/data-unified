[Victories](victories.md) temporarily increase a hero's power during an adventure, but Experience (abbreviated "XP") permanently improves their capabilities. Each time you finish a [respite](respite.md) (see below), you gain XP equal to your [Victories](victories.md), then your [Victories](victories.md) reset to 0. In other words, your [Victories](victories.md) are converted to XP when you finish a [respite](respite.md).

For more information on how XP increases your hero's power, see Heroic Advancement in [Making a Hero](../../chapter/making-a-hero.md).
