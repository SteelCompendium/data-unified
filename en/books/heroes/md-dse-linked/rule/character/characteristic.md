Each creature in the game has five characteristics that represent their physical and mental prowess.

#### Characteristic Scores

Each [characteristic](characteristic.md) has a score that runs from −5 to +5. The higher a score, the more impact a creature has with that [characteristic](characteristic.md). A baby bunny rabbit would have a [Might](might.md) score of −5, while an ancient dragon would have a [Might](might.md) score of 5. The average [human](../../ancestry/human.md) has a score of 0 in all their [characteristics](characteristic.md). [Characteristic](characteristic.md) scores are added to [power rolls](../dice/power-roll.md)—the dice rolls you make whenever your character attempts a task with an uncertain outcome (see [Power Rolls](../dice/power-roll.md) below).
