*I stabbed the wyrm Axarthan in the heart and their silver blood washed over me, leaving me... as you see.*

**Prerequisite:** You defeat a leader or solo creature with the Dragon keyword, such as a gloom dragon.

**Effect:** Choose one of the following benefits:

- *Dragon Scaled:* Dragon scales grow on your body wherever the heart's blood of the dragon touched you. You gain the Wyrmplate signature trait from the [dragon knight](../ancestry/dragon-knight.md) ancestry (see Chapter 3: [Ancestries](../chapter/ancestries.md)).
- *Dragon Touched:* You gain 3 ancestry points to spend on purchased [dragon knight](../ancestry/dragon-knight.md) [ancestry traits](../chapter/ancestries.md).
