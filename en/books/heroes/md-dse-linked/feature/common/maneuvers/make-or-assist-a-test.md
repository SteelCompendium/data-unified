effects:
    - effect: |-
        Many [tests](../../../rule/test/test.md) are maneuvers if made in combat. Searching a chest with a [Reason](../../../rule/character/reason.md) [test](../../../rule/test/test.md), picking a door's lock with an [Agility](../../../rule/character/agility.md) [test](../../../rule/test/test.md), or lifting a portcullis with a [Might](../../../rule/character/might.md) [test](../../../rule/test/test.md) would all be maneuvers. Assisting a [test](../../../rule/test/test.md) is also a maneuver in combat (see Assist a [Test](../../../rule/test/test.md) in Chapter 9: [Tests](../../../chapter/tests.md)).

        Complex or time-consuming [tests](../../../rule/test/test.md) might require a main action if made in combat—or could take so long that they can't be made during combat at all. Other [tests](../../../rule/test/test.md) that take no time at all, such as a [Reason](../../../rule/character/reason.md) [test](../../../rule/test/test.md) to recall lore about mummies, are usually [free maneuvers](../../../rule/combat/free-maneuver.md) in combat. The Director has the final say regarding which [tests](../../../rule/test/test.md) can be made as maneuvers.
