effects:
    - effect: |-
        When a creature takes the [Charge](charge.md) main action, they move up to their [speed](../../../rule/character/speed.md) in a straight line, then make a [melee](../../../rule/combat/melee.md) [free strike](free-strike.md) (see [Free Strikes](free-strike.md) below) against a target when they end their move. If the creature has an ability with the Charge keyword, they can use that ability against the target instead of a [free strike](free-strike.md).

        A creature can't move through [difficult terrain](../../../movement/difficult-terrain.md) or [shift](../../../movement/shifting.md) when they charge. They can [fly](../../../movement/fly.md) or [burrow](../../../movement/burrow.md) as part of the [Charge](charge.md) main action if they have that movement available to them, but they can't climb or swim while charging unless they can automatically use that movement at full [speed](../../../rule/character/speed.md).
