Whenever you fail a [Might](../rule/character/might.md) [test](../rule/test/test.md), you can lose [Stamina](../rule/health/stamina.md) equal to 1d6 + your level to improve the outcome of the [test](../rule/test/test.md) by one tier. You can use this perk only once per [test](../rule/test/test.md).
