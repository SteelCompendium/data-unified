Your mind is an encyclopedia, though not always an easy one to organize. When you finish a [respite](../rule/resource/respite.md), choose one skill from the [lore skill group](../skill/group/lore.md) that you don't have. You have that skill until you finish your next [respite](../rule/resource/respite.md). Additionally, if you spend 1 uninterrupted minute or more reading any page of text, you can memorize its contents, allowing you to memorize entire books with sufficient time.
