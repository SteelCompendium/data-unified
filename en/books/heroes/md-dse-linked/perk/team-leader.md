At the start of a [group test](../rule/test/group-test.md) or [montage test](../rule/test/montage-test.md), you can spend a [hero token](../rule/resource/hero-token.md). If you do, all participants make [tests](../rule/test/test.md) as if they also had any skill you have from the [exploration group](../skill/group/exploration.md).
