When a creature is [frightened](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/condition/frightened), any [ability roll](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/rule.dice/ability-roll) they make against the source of their fear takes a [bane](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/rule.dice/bane). If that source is a creature, their [ability rolls](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/rule.dice/ability-roll) made against the [frightened](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/condition/frightened) creature gain an [edge](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/rule.dice/edge). A [frightened](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/condition/frightened) creature can't willingly move closer to the source of their fear if they know the location of that source. If a creature gains the [frightened](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/condition/frightened) [condition](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/rule.combat/condition) from one source while already [frightened](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/condition/frightened) by a different source, the new [condition](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/rule.combat/condition) replaces the old one.
