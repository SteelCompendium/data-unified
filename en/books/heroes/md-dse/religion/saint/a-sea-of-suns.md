**Domains:** Creation, Life

A Sea of Suns, also known as the Composer.

Credited with inventing harmony, it is said that after she discovered the power of blending many voices into one, the elves sang for an entire century uninterrupted1. The harmonics so complicated they created new beings like faeries, dryads, and the elgenwights.

The Composer discovered, or invented, the power of music to manipulate reality. Her [troubadours](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/class/troubadour), it was said, could return the dead to life. "In the music, you can live forever." Legend has it she was at the battle of Kalas Valiar when the first Army of Night besieged its walls. When their corruption engines [weakened](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/condition/weakened) the impenetrable walls, she stood alone on the parapets and sang. For forty days did her voice bolster the walls, stemming the flood of evil. Her song ended only when her life was taken by A Heart Trapped in Amber, the sorcerer-assassin of the star elves.

_1. If this story wasn't invented by the [dwarves](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/ancestry/dwarf) or [humans](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/ancestry/human), it must at least have passed through one of their cultures. The celestials did not reckon time the way younger species do and would not have said "for an entire century."_
