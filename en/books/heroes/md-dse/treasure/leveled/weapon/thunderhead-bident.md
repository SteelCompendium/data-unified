*This bident is made from two pieces of moon metal twisted together, and hums like a tuning fork.*

**Keywords:** Magic, Medium Weapon

**[Item Prerequisite](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/rule.downtime/item-prerequisite):** A jar of captured thunder, two ingots of moon metal

**[Project Source](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/rule.downtime/project-source):** Texts or lore in Zaliac

**[Project Roll](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/rule.downtime/project-roll) [Characteristic](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/rule.character/characteristic):** [Might](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/rule.character/might), [Reason](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/rule.character/reason), or [Intuition](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/rule.character/intuition)

**Project Goal:** 450

**1st Level:** Any weapon ability that deals [rolled damage](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/rule.damage/rolled-damage) using this weapon deals an extra 1 sonic damage. Additionally, when the weapon is used with any ability that [pushes](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/movement/forced-movement) a target, you gain a +1 [bonus](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/rule.dice/bonuses-and-penalties) to the [forced movement](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/movement/forced-movement) [distance](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/rule.combat/distance). If the weapon is used with a damage-dealing ability that doesn't impose [forced movement](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/movement/forced-movement), you can [push](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/movement/forced-movement) the target 1 square.

**5th Level:** The weapon's extra sonic damage increases to 2, and the additional [distance](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/rule.combat/distance) or [distance](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/rule.combat/distance) of a [push](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/movement/forced-movement) for abilities using the weapon increases to 2 squares. Additionally, the weapon can be used with [ranged](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/rule.combat/ranged) weapon abilities, and gains power the farther it is hurled. For each 2 squares the weapon travels to the target of a [ranged](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/rule.combat/ranged) [strike](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/rule.combat/strike), the [strike](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/rule.combat/strike) deals an extra 1 sonic damage.

**9th Level:** The weapon's extra sonic damage increases to 3, and it deals an extra 1 sonic damage for each square it travels as part of a [ranged](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/rule.combat/ranged) [strike](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/rule.combat/strike). Additionally, whenever you make a weapon [strike](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/rule.combat/strike) using this weapon, each enemy [adjacent](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/rule.combat/adjacent) to the target takes 6 sonic damage.
