You made a deal with a [devil](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/ancestry/devil). Not a very good deal, because it wasn't a very good [devil](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/ancestry/devil). It's too late for regrets, though, because your soul is forfeit unless you find a loophole or can convince the [devil](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/ancestry/devil) to void the deal.

**Benefit:** Choose one of the following benefits:

- You earn 2 [Renown](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/rule.resource/renown).
- Your [Wealth](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/rule.resource/wealth) increases by 2.
- You gain a +3 [bonus](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/rule.dice/bonuses-and-penalties) to [Stamina](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/rule.health/stamina).

**Drawback:** Your body bears a fiendish mark. Any creature who understands religion and notes the mark can tell that your soul belongs to Hell, imposing a [bane](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/rule.dice/bane) on any [test](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/rule.test/test) you make to interact with those creatures (unless they're into that). Additionally, when you die, your soul goes to Hell and you can't be restored to life.
