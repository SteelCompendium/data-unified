Your siblings each inherited money or land, but you received a strange, seemingly useless [trinket](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/rule.treasure/trinket)—along with the advice that maybe you weren't cut out for an ordinary, peaceful life.

**Benefit:** You have a somewhat inoperative 2nd-[echelon](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/rule.general/echelon) [trinket](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/rule.treasure/trinket) of the Director's choice (see Treasures in Chapter 13: [Rewards](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/chapter/rewards)). This [trinket](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/rule.treasure/trinket) functions only while the total of your level plus your [Victories](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/rule.resource/victories) is 5 or higher. You don't learn what the [trinket](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/rule.treasure/trinket)'s powers are until the first time it becomes operative.

**Drawback:** With no other inheritance, you accumulated debts. The first time your [Wealth](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/rule.resource/wealth) exceeds 1, you lose 1 [Wealth](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/rule.resource/wealth).
