You came into contact with a mote of pure chaos energy, or were subjected to a [supernatural](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/rule.general/supernatural) effect or object that fused chaos into your very being. Now you can sprout and retract your limbs in a way that horrifies unprepared onlookers.

**Benefit:** You gain an [edge](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/rule.dice/edge) on the [Escape Grab](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/feature.common.maneuvers/escape-grab), [Grab](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/feature.common.maneuvers/grab), and [Knockback](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/feature.common.maneuvers/knockback) maneuvers. Additionally, you can hold an additional item even when your hands are full.

**Drawback:** While [dying](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/rule.health/dying), you grow and retract uncoordinated limbs at random, imposing a [bane](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/rule.dice/bane) on your [power rolls](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/rule.dice/power-roll).
