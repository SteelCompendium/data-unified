You were caught in a storm and struck by lightning—but something saved you from death. Perhaps it was a gods—given miracle, a latent psionic gift, or the magic of a helpful [elementalist](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/class/elementalist), but you absorbed the lightning into your body. It's always there now, simmering under the surface.

**Benefit:** Whenever you regain [Stamina](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/rule.health/stamina) in combat, you gain 1 [surge](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/rule.resource/surge). Whenever you spend a [surge](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/rule.resource/surge) to deal extra damage, you can make that extra damage into lightning damage.

**Drawback:** Whenever you are wet, you have [damage weakness](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/rule.damage/damage-weakness) 5.
