You were apprenticed to learn a crafting trade. Your mentor said you had a special gift and might well become a master of your craft someday. But before your training was complete, your mentor was killed.

**Benefit:** You have one skill of your choice from the [crafting skill group](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/skill.group/crafting). Additionally, choose one of your skills from the [crafting group](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/skill.group/crafting). You gain an [edge](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/rule.dice/edge) on any [test](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/rule.test/test) that uses that skill.

**Drawback:** Whoever killed your mentor cursed you. You take a [bane](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/rule.dice/bane) on any [test](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/rule.test/test) that doesn't use one of your skills.
