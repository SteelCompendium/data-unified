You're secretly important—but it's not safe for your true identity to be known. Perhaps you're the witness to a crime or a member of a royal family on the run from a usurper. Until you are no longer at risk of being [hunted](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/complication/hunted), you'll maintain the guise of an ordinary adventurer.

**Benefit:** You have a skill of your choice from the [intrigue skill group](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/skill.group/intrigue). Additionally, you can resume your true identity temporarily. While in your true identity, your [Renown](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/rule.resource/renown) and [Wealth](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/rule.resource/wealth) are treated as 2 higher than usual, and you might gain other benefits in consultation with the Director.

**Drawback:** Each time you resume your true identity while you are still [hunted](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/complication/hunted), you have a 20 percent cumulative chance each day that your enemies will find you. This chance resets if you resume your [secret identity](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/complication/secret-identity) for 1 day.
