In a sinister ritual, your soul has been bound to that of another creature. This might be a companion, a creature you are beholden to, or an enemy. When they die, you die—making you the perfect bodyguard.

**Benefit:** Choose another creature who doesn't have the [Lifebonded](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/complication/lifebonded) complication. When you die, your body disappears until that creature finishes a [respite](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/rule.resource/respite) or earns 1 or more [Victories](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/rule.resource/victories). You then appear next to the creature, fully healed.

**Drawback:** If the creature you're bound to dies, you die as well, no matter what other traits or features you have.
