As a child, you survived a gnoll attack. But that attack left you with a jagged scar and the occasional fit of bloodlust.

**Benefit:** Whenever an ally within 5 squares is reduced to 0 [Stamina](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/rule.health/stamina), you can use a [triggered action](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/rule.combat/triggered-action) to move up to your [speed](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/rule.character/speed) and make a [free strike](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/feature.common.main-actions/free-strike).

**Drawback:** While you are [dazed](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/condition/dazed), if you start your [turn](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/rule.combat/turn) [adjacent](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/rule.combat/adjacent) to one or more creatures, you must use your main action to make a [melee](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/rule.combat/melee) [free strike](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/feature.common.main-actions/free-strike) against an [adjacent](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/rule.combat/adjacent) creature.

**Special:** You can't take this complication if you can't be made [dazed](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/condition/dazed).
