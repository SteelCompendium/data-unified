An **edge** represents a situational advantage a hero or an enemy has when making a power roll. For example, a standing hero who makes a [melee](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/rule.combat/melee) [strike](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/rule.combat/strike) against a [prone](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/condition/prone) creature gains an edge on the power roll for their [strike](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/rule.combat/strike). A pair of magic gloves that makes your hands sticky might grant you an edge when making a power roll to climb walls!

When you make a power roll with an edge, you gain a +2 [bonus](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/rule.dice/bonuses-and-penalties) to the roll. If you make a power roll with two or more edges, you have a **double edge**. With a double edge, you don't add anything to the power roll, but the outcome of the roll automatically improves one tier (to a maximum of tier 3).
