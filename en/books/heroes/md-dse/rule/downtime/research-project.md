Heroes can undertake many different types of research downtime projects, which can involve seeking out new lore, improving existing knowledge with study, uncovering rumors or secrets, and more.

Unless a project has an event table of its own or a special event entry, the Director uses the Crafting and Research Events table for research [project events](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/rule.downtime/project-event).
