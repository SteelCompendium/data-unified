Crafting projects enable heroes to create vehicles, [supernatural](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/rule.general/supernatural) treasures, and more. When you start a crafting project, other creatures can also work on the project, using their [respite](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/rule.resource/respite) activity to contribute a [project roll](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/rule.downtime/project-roll) in order to get the work done faster.

Unless a project has an event table of its own, the Director uses the Crafting and Research Events table for crafting [project events](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/rule.downtime/project-event).
