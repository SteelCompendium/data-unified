Unless otherwise noted, a character of any of these ancestries is [size](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/rule.character/size) 1M and has speed 5 and [stability](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/rule.character/stability) 0.

Where an ancestry provides you with an ability, see Abilities in Chapter 5: [Classes](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/chapter/classes) for details of the ability format.
