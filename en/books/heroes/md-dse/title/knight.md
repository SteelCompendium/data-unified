*Kneel, heroes. Arise, knights of Tor, and may your swords be ever sharp in our service.*

**Prerequisite:** A [noble](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/title/noble) or [monarch](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/title/monarch) grants you knighthood or a similar rank.

**Effect:** Choose one of the following benefits:

- *Heraldic Fame:* You earn 1 [Renown](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/rule.resource/renown).
- *Knightly Aegis:* Your [Stamina](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/rule.health/stamina) maximum increases by 6.
- *Knightly Challenge:* You have the following ability, which can be paid for using the [Heroic Resource](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/rule.resource/heroic-resource) of your class.

##### Knightly Challenge (5 Heroic Resource)

>
> *Have at thee!*
>
> | **[Melee](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/rule.combat/melee), [Strike](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/rule.combat/strike), Weapon** |     **Main action** |
> |---------------------------|--------------------:|
> | **📏 [Melee](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/rule.combat/melee) 1**            | **🎯 One creature** |
>
> **[Power Roll](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/rule.dice/power-roll) + [Might](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/rule.character/might) or [Agility](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/rule.character/agility):**
>
> - **≤11:** 7 damage; [taunted](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/condition/taunted) (save ends)
> - **12-16:** 11 damage; [taunted](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/condition/taunted) (save ends)
> - **17+:** 16 damage; [taunted](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/condition/taunted) (save ends)
>
> **Effect:** You can end the [taunted](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/condition/taunted) [condition](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/rule.combat/condition) on the target as a [free maneuver](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/rule.combat/free-maneuver).
>
> **Special:** If you take this title, you might occasionally be called upon to perform duties for the person who knighted you.
