*Your coin won't spend here. The Heroes of Gravesford drink for free in this tavern!*

**Prerequisite:** You save a community from certain destruction.

**Effect:** Choose one of the following benefits:

- *Easy Marks:* You gain an [edge](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/rule.dice/edge) on [tests](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/rule.test/test) made using skills from the [interpersonal](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/skill.group/interpersonal) and [intrigue](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/skill.group/intrigue) skill groups when influencing members of a community that you have saved.
- *Local Fame:* You earn 1 [Renown](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/rule.resource/renown).
- *A New Dawn:* Each time you finish a [respite](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/rule.resource/respite) while in a community you have saved, the party gains a [hero token](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/rule.resource/hero-token). This [hero token](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/rule.resource/hero-token) disappears at the end of your next [respite](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/rule.resource/respite) if it hasn't been used.

> **[Renown](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/rule.resource/renown) and [Wealth](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/rule.resource/wealth)**
>
> Many titles bestow [Renown](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/rule.resource/renown), [followers](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/rule.general/follower), and wealth upon heroes. Those topics are covered later in this chapter.
