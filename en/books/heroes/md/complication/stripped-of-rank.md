You were trained as an officer, but you no longer serve. Whether you fled from a battle, were dishonorably discharged, or defected from an evil army, you make your own way in the world now—though your military training will never truly leave you.

**Benefit:** You have the following ability.

> ###### Issue Order
>
> *"Move or die, folks."*
>
> | **[Ranged](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/rule.combat/ranged)**       | **[Main action](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/rule.combat/turn)** |
> |------------------|----------------:|
> | **📏 [Ranged](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/rule.combat/ranged) 10** | **🎯 One ally** |
>
> **Effect:** The target can use a [triggered action](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/rule.combat/triggered-action) to take a main action, a maneuver, or a move action.
>
> **Special:** If you have the [Strike Now](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/feature.ability.tactician.level-1/strike-now) [tactician](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/class/tactician) ability, the target can use a free [triggered action](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/rule.combat/triggered-action) instead of a [triggered action](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/rule.combat/triggered-action) to gain the benefit of this ability.

**Drawback:** Rather than attracting [followers](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/rule.general/follower) at 3, 6, 9, and 12 [Renown](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/rule.resource/renown), you can attract [followers](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/rule.general/follower) only when your [Renown](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/rule.resource/renown) reaches 4, 8, 12, and 16. See [Renown](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/rule.resource/renown) in Chapter 13: [Rewards](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/chapter/rewards).
