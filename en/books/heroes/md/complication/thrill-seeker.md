You live for danger. Whether in battle or [mundane](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/complication/mundane) peril, you can transcend your usual limits—and once you've tasted that excitement, you want more.

**Benefit:** Each time your party reaches 2, 4, and 6 [Victories](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/rule.resource/victories), you earn the party a [hero token](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/rule.resource/hero-token).

**Drawback:** At the start of a new game session, the party doesn't earn a [hero token](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/rule.resource/hero-token) for your character.
