You've always been a lone wolf. With no one else to lean on, you've picked up a million survival tricks. Which made it all the more surprising when you joined your current adventuring group and found the family you'd never known you needed.

**Benefit:** When you finish a [respite](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/rule.resource/respite), choose a skill you don't have. You have that skill until the end of your next [respite](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/rule.resource/respite).

**Drawback:** Now that you finally have people who care about you, you won't let anyone take them away! Whenever a creature reduces one of your allies to 0 [Stamina](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/rule.health/stamina), you are [taunted](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/condition/taunted) by that creature until your ally's [Stamina](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/rule.health/stamina) is higher than 0, another creature makes you [taunted](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/condition/taunted), or the end of the encounter.
