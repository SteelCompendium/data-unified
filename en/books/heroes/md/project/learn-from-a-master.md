When you seek to learn from a master, you choose the goal you wish to work on as well as the benefit you would gain from it. The Director tells you whether the master or the materials they've left behind are able to teach you what you want to learn.

##### Acquire Ability

You gain one [signature ability](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/rule.combat/signature-ability) of your choice from the master's class (gaining a second [signature ability](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/rule.combat/signature-ability) if the master is of the same class as you). If the master is a [tactician](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/class/tactician), you can gain the [Strike Now](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/feature.ability.tactician.level-1/strike-now) ability instead. You can't gain this benefit again for the same class.

##### Hone Ability

You sharpen the effectiveness of one of your abilities of your choice. Choose between adding a +1/+1/+1 damage [bonus](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/rule.dice/bonuses-and-penalties) to the ability, or improving the [distance](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/rule.combat/distance) of a [ranged](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/rule.combat/ranged) ability by 2. An ability can be honed only once this way.

##### Improve Control

You learn to use one of your [heroic abilities](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/rule.general/heroic-ability) more efficiently, reducing its baseline [Heroic Resource](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/rule.resource/heroic-resource) cost by 1 (to a minimum of 1). An ability can only be improved this way once.
