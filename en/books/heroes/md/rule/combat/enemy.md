name: Enemy
scc: mcdm.heroes.v1/rule.combat/enemy
type: rule
---

If an ability targets one or more [enemies](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/rule.combat/enemy), it can affect only creatures who are hostile to the creature using the ability. Typically, you decide who counts as an enemy for the purpose of using your hero's abilities, though the Director has the final say.
