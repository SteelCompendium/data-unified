---
name: Cover
scc: mcdm.heroes.v1/rule.combat/cover
type: rule
---

When you have [line of effect](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/rule.combat/line-of-effect) to a creature or object but that target has at least half their form blocked by a solid object such as a tree, wall, or overturned table, the target has cover. You take a [bane](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/rule.dice/bane) on damage-dealing abilities used against creatures or objects that have cover from you.
