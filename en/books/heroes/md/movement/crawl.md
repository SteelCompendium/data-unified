If you [are prone](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/condition/prone) (see [Conditions](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/rule.combat/condition) in Chapter 5: [Classes](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/chapter/classes)), you can remain prone and [crawl](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/movement/crawl) on the ground. Doing so costs you 1 additional square of movement for every square you [crawl](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/movement/crawl). If you intentionally want to [crawl](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/movement/crawl), you can [fall prone](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/condition/prone) as a [free maneuver](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/rule.combat/free-maneuver). While voluntarily prone, you can choose to stand as a [free maneuver](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/rule.combat/free-maneuver).
