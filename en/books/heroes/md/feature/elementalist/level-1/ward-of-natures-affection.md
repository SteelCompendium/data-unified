The green energy writhing within your body allows you to produce powerful vines when you're in danger. Whenever a creature within a number of squares equal to your [Reason](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/rule.character/reason) score deals damage to you, you can use a free [triggered action](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/rule.combat/triggered-action) to [slide](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/movement/forced-movement) that creature up to a number of squares equal to your [Reason](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/rule.character/reason) score.
