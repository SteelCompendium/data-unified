You rely on a unique blend of martial techniques and psionic prowess to take down your foes and defend your allies.

##### Signature Abilities

Choose two [signature abilities](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/rule.combat/signature-ability) from the following options. [Signature abilities](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/rule.combat/signature-ability) can be used at will. (*Quick Build:* [Faster Than the Eye](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/feature.ability.null.level-1/faster-than-the-eye), [Inertial Step](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/feature.ability.null.level-1/inertial-step).)

##### Heroic Abilities

You have mastered a range of [heroic abilities](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/rule.general/heroic-ability), all of them channeled through your discipline.

###### 3-Discipline Ability

Choose one [heroic ability](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/rule.general/heroic-ability) from the following options, each of which costs 3 discipline to use. (*Quick Build:* [Chronal Spike](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/feature.ability.null.level-1/chronal-spike).)

###### 5-Discipline Ability

Choose one [heroic ability](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/rule.general/heroic-ability) from the following options, each of which costs 5 discipline to use. (*Quick Build:* [A Squad Unto Myself](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/feature.ability.null.level-1/a-squad-unto-myself).)
