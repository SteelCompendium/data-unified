You have an epic resource called [applause](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/feature.troubadour.level-10/applause). Each time you finish a [respite](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/rule.resource/respite), you gain [applause](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/feature.troubadour.level-10/applause) equal to the [XP](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/rule.resource/experience) you gain. You can spend [applause](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/feature.troubadour.level-10/applause) on your abilities as if it were drama.

Additionally, whenever you or a creature within 3 squares would obtain a failure or a tier 1 outcome on a [test](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/rule.test/test), you can spend 1 [applause](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/feature.troubadour.level-10/applause) to improve the outcome by 1 tier.

[Applause](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/feature.troubadour.level-10/applause) remains until you spend it.
