Whenever you obtain a success on a [test](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/rule.test/test), each [NPC](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/rule.general/npc) within your [line of effect](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/rule.combat/line-of-effect) has their Impression score decreased by 4 during a negotiation (to a minimum of 1), and each ally within 3 squares of you gains an [edge](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/rule.dice/edge) on their next [test](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/rule.test/test). These effects last until you start your next [respite](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/rule.resource/respite).
