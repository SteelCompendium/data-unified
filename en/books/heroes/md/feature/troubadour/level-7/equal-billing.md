You can use your [Scene Partner](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/feature.troubadour.level-1/scene-partner) feature to form a bond with one willing hero instead of an [NPC](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/rule.general/npc) you interact with using a [test](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/rule.test/test). If you bond with another hero, you lose your existing bond with a hero.

Additionally, you and creatures you are bonded with gain a +1 [bonus](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/rule.dice/bonuses-and-penalties) to [saving throws](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/rule.general/saving-throw). Whenever you or a bonded creature succeeds on a [saving throw](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/rule.general/saving-throw), you and each creature you are bonded with gains [temporary Stamina](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/rule.health/temporary-stamina) equal to your level.
