You surround yourself with an invisible ward of telekinetic energy. Whenever an [adjacent](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/rule.combat/adjacent) creature deals damage to you, you can use a free [triggered action](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/rule.combat/triggered-action) to [push](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/movement/forced-movement) them up to a number of squares equal to your [Reason](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/rule.character/reason) score.
