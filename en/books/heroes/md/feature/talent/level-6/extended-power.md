If the ability is [ranged](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/rule.combat/ranged), the [distance](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/rule.combat/distance) gains a [bonus](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/rule.dice/bonuses-and-penalties) equal to your [Reason](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/rule.character/reason) score. If the ability is [melee](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/rule.combat/melee), the [distance](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/rule.combat/distance) gains a +2 [bonus](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/rule.dice/bonuses-and-penalties).
