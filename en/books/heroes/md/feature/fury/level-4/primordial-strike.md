You can manifest your ferocity directly as an elemental force created by the Primordial Chaos. As part of any strike, you can spend 1 ferocity to gain 1 [surge](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/rule.resource/surge) that must be used for that [strike](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/rule.combat/strike). The extra damage dealt by the [surge](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/rule.resource/surge) can be acid, cold, corruption, fire, lightning, poison, or sonic (your choice).
