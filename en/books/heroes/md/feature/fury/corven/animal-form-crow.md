While you are in your crow form, your [size](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/rule.character/size) is 1T and you can [fly](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/movement/fly). You can use the [Hide](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/feature.common.maneuvers/hide) maneuver as a [free maneuver](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/rule.combat/free-maneuver), and you can use your allies as [cover](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/rule.combat/cover) when you hide. You can't use any abilities while in this form except for [Aspect of the Wild](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/feature.ability.fury.stormwight-kits/aspect-of-the-wild).
