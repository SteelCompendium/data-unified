As your ferocity grows, you gain benefits as noted on the [Boren](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/kit/boren) [Growing Ferocity](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/feature.fury.boren/growing-ferocity) table. Benefits are cumulative except where an improved benefit replaces a lesser benefit.

###### Boren Growing Ferocity Table

| Ferocity        | Benefit                                                                                                                                                                                                                   |
|-----------------|---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| 2               | You can have up to two creatures [grabbed](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/condition/grabbed) at a time. Additionally, whenever you make a [strike](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/rule.combat/strike) against a creature you have [grabbed](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/condition/grabbed), you gain 1 [surge](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/rule.resource/surge).                                                                       |
| 4               | The first time you grab a creature on a [turn](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/rule.combat/turn), you gain 1 [surge](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/rule.resource/surge).                                                                                                                                                           |
| 6               | You gain an [edge](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/rule.dice/edge) on the [Grab](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/feature.common.maneuvers/grab) and [Knockback](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/feature.common.maneuvers/knockback) maneuvers.                                                                                                                                                                     |
| 8 (4th level)   | The first time you grab a creature on a [turn](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/rule.combat/turn), you gain 2 [surges](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/rule.resource/surge) instead of 1.                                                                                                                                             |
| 10 (7th level)  | You have a double [edge](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/rule.dice/edge) on the [Grab](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/feature.common.maneuvers/grab) and [Knockback](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/feature.common.maneuvers/knockback) maneuvers.                                                                                                                                                               |
| 12 (10th level) | Whenever you use a [heroic ability](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/rule.general/heroic-ability), you gain 10 [temporary Stamina](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/rule.health/temporary-stamina). Additionally, whenever you have a creature [grabbed](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/condition/grabbed), any [ability roll](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/rule.dice/ability-roll) made against that creature gains a [bonus](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/rule.dice/bonuses-and-penalties) to its [potency](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/rule.character/potency) equal to your [Might](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/rule.character/might) score. |
