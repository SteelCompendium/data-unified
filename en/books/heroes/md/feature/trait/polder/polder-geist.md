Evading others' notice gives you freedom to move. At the start of each of your [turns](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/rule.combat/turn) during combat, if no enemy has [line of effect](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/rule.combat/line-of-effect) to you or if you are hidden from or have [concealment](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/rule.combat/concealment) from any enemy with [line of effect](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/rule.combat/line-of-effect) to you, you gain a +3 [bonus](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/rule.dice/bonuses-and-penalties) to [speed](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/rule.character/speed) until the end of your [turn](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/rule.combat/turn).
