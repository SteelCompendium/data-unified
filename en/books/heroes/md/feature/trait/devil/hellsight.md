Your eyes let you see through darkness, fog, and other obscuring effects. You don't take a [bane](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/rule.dice/bane) on [strikes](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/rule.combat/strike) made against creatures with [concealment](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/rule.combat/concealment).
