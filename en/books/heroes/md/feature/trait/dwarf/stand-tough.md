Your body is made to withstand the blows of your enemies. Your [Might](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/rule.character/might) score is treated as 1 higher for the purpose of resisting [potencies](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/rule.character/potency), and you gain an [edge](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/rule.dice/edge) on [Might](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/rule.character/might) [tests](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/rule.test/test) when called for to resist environmental effects or a creature's traits or abilities.
