*You shout a word of power that allows you to rewrite reality to your whims.*

| **Magic**   | **[Maneuver](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/rule.combat/turn)** |
|-------------|-------------:|
| **📏 Self** |  **🎯 Self** |

**Effect:** Until the end of the encounter, whenever you deal [rolled damage](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/rule.damage/rolled-damage) to a creature or object, or enable a creature to spend a [Recovery](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/rule.health/recoveries), you can use a free [triggered action](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/rule.combat/triggered-action) to give that creature or object one of the following effects until the start of your next [turn](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/rule.combat/turn). If this ability is triggered by multiple targets taking damage or multiple creatures spending [Recoveries](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/rule.health/recoveries) simultaneously, each target receives the same effect:

- The target has [damage weakness](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/rule.damage/damage-weakness) equal to your [Presence](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/rule.character/presence) score against any magic, psionic, or weapon ability.
- The target has [damage immunity](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/rule.damage/damage-immunity) equal to your [Presence](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/rule.character/presence) score.
- The target has a [bonus](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/rule.dice/bonuses-and-penalties) to [stability](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/rule.character/stability) and a [penalty](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/rule.dice/bonuses-and-penalties) to [speed](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/rule.character/speed) equal to your [Presence](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/rule.character/presence) score.
- The target has a [bonus](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/rule.dice/bonuses-and-penalties) to [speed](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/rule.character/speed) and a [penalty](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/rule.dice/bonuses-and-penalties) to [stability](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/rule.character/stability) equal to your [Presence](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/rule.character/presence) score.
