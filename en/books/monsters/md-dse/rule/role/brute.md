Brutes are hardy creatures who have lots of [Stamina](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/rule.health/stamina) and deal lots of damage. They have abilities and [traits](scc.v1:mcdm.monsters.v1/rule.monster/monster-trait) that make them difficult to ignore and hard to get away from, and that let them push enemies around.
