A leader is a powerful monster who buffs their allies and grants them additional actions. They utilize [villain actions](scc.v1:mcdm.monsters.v1/rule.monster/villain-action) and can stand toe-to-toe with two or more heroes of the same level by themself. Typically, only one leader appears in a battle at a time, alongside [minions](scc.v1:mcdm.monsters.v1/rule.organization/minion), [horde](scc.v1:mcdm.monsters.v1/rule.organization/horde) or [platoon](scc.v1:mcdm.monsters.v1/rule.organization/platoon) creatures, and [elites](scc.v1:mcdm.monsters.v1/rule.organization/elite). Leader creatures have no additional creature role (see Creature Roles).
