Any non-[Mount](scc.v1:mcdm.monsters.v1/rule.role/mount), non-minion creature, who speaks a language that a [squad](scc.v1:mcdm.monsters.v1/rule.monster/squad) of [minions](scc.v1:mcdm.monsters.v1/rule.organization/minion) can understand can be attached to that squad as a captain. Captains aren't necessarily strategic leaders with brilliant tactics. Sometimes they're just powerful creatures who bully, inspire, or have some supernatural influence that drives other creatures to action.

A squad of minions can have only one captain, and a creature can't be captain to more than one squad of minions.

##### Separate Actions and Stamina

A [captain](scc.v1:mcdm.monsters.v1/rule.monster/captain) takes their [turn](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/rule.combat/turn) at the same time as the minion members of their [squad](scc.v1:mcdm.monsters.v1/rule.monster/squad) but isn't limited in their action options as minions are. A captain's [Stamina](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/rule.health/stamina) isn't added to a minion squad's Stamina pool, and is tracked as for any other creature in combat.

##### Captain Benefits

While a minion [squad](scc.v1:mcdm.monsters.v1/rule.monster/squad) has a [captain](scc.v1:mcdm.monsters.v1/rule.monster/captain), each minion in the squad gains the benefits noted at the "With Captain" entry on their stat block. Usually, this benefit is either a damage boost, a bonus to [speed](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/rule.character/speed), or additional [Stamina](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/rule.health/stamina).

##### I Am the Captain Now

If a [squad](scc.v1:mcdm.monsters.v1/rule.monster/squad) of [minions](scc.v1:mcdm.monsters.v1/rule.organization/minion) loses their [captain](scc.v1:mcdm.monsters.v1/rule.monster/captain), a new allied creature can become that squad's captain at the start of the next [round](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/rule.combat/combat-round) (no action required).
