---
name: End Effect
scc: mcdm.monsters.v1/rule.monster/end-effect
type: rule
---

Certain creatures have the ability to take damage in order to end one effect on them that can be ended by a [saving throw](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/rule.general/saving-throw). The damage the creature takes to end an effect can't be reduced in any way.
