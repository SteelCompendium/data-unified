---
name: Signature Abilities
scc: mcdm.heroes.v1/rule.combat/signature-ability
type: rule
---

Some abilities granted by your class, kit, and other parts of the game are signature abilities. The rules specifically state when an ability is a signature ability. Signature abilities don't require your [Heroic Resource](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/rule.resource/heroic-resource) to use, but sometimes let you spend your [Heroic Resource](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/rule.resource/heroic-resource) to enhance or add to their effects.
