---
name: Suffocating
scc: mcdm.heroes.v1/rule.health/suffocating
type: rule
---

During combat or under similarly stressful circumstances, you can hold your breath for a number of [combat rounds](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/rule.combat/combat-round) equal to your [Might](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/rule.character/might) score (minimum 1 round). At the end of each [combat round](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/rule.combat/combat-round) after that, you take 1d6 damage while holding your breath.

Out of combat, you can hold your breath for a number of minutes equal to your [Might](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/rule.character/might) score. Being unable to breathe after that time counts as a stressful [condition](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/rule.combat/condition), causing you to run out of air as above.
