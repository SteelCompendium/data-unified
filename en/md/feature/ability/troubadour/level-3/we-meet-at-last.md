*You magically intertwine your fate with another creature—for better or worse.*

| **Magic, [Ranged](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/rule.combat/ranged)** |        **Maneuver** |
|-------------------|--------------------:|
| **📏 [Ranged](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/rule.combat/ranged) 10**  | **🎯 One creature** |

**Effect:** Until the end of the encounter, both you and the target can target each other with abilities even if you are beyond [distance](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/rule.combat/distance), with the [distance](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/rule.combat/distance) of this ability replacing those abilities' [distances](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/rule.combat/distance). The target can't be [force moved](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/movement/forced-movement) by an ability used beyond [distance](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/rule.combat/distance) this way.

Additionally, once on each of your [turns](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/rule.combat/turn), you can use a [free maneuver](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/rule.combat/free-maneuver) to communicate a motivating or dispiriting message to the target, either granting them 2 [surges](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/rule.resource/surge) or forcing them to take a [bane](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/rule.dice/bane) on the next [ability roll](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/rule.dice/ability-roll) they make before the start of your next [turn](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/rule.combat/turn).
