*You project an [aura](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/rule.combat/aura) that dampens the power of your foes.*

| **Area, Psionic** |                  **Maneuver** |
|-------------------|------------------------------:|
| **📏 1 [aura](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/rule.combat/aura)**     | **🎯 Each enemy in the area** |

**Effect:** Each target reduces their [potencies](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/rule.character/potency) by 1.

Once as a [free maneuver](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/rule.combat/free-maneuver) on each of your [turns](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/rule.combat/turn), you can spend 1 discipline and give your [Null Field](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/feature.null.level-1/null-field) one of the following additional effects until the start of your next [turn](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/rule.combat/turn):

- Gravitic Disruption: The first time on a [turn](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/rule.combat/turn) that a target takes damage, you can [slide](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/movement/forced-movement) them up to 2 squares.
- Inertial Anchor: Any target who starts their [turn](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/rule.combat/turn) in the area can't [shift](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/movement/shifting).
- Synaptic Break: Whenever you or any ally uses an ability against a target that has a [potency](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/rule.character/potency) effect, the [potency](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/rule.character/potency) is increased by 1.

This ability remains active even after an encounter ends. It ends only if you are [dying](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/rule.health/dying) or if you willingly end it (no action required).
