A creature can use the [Stand Up](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/feature.common.maneuvers/stand-up) maneuver to stand up if they [are prone](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/condition/prone), ending that [condition](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/rule.combat/condition). Alternatively, they can use this maneuver to make a willing [adjacent](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/rule.combat/adjacent) prone creature stand up.
