Your god blesses your flesh and infuses it with divine quickness. You gain a +1 [bonus](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/rule.dice/bonuses-and-penalties) to [speed](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/rule.character/speed) and to the [distance](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/rule.combat/distance) you can [shift](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/movement/shifting) when you take the [Disengage](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/feature.common.move-actions/disengage) move action.
