By bragging, intimidating, leading, or lying, you attempt to spread one piece of information into the local area. Make a [Presence](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/rule.character/presence) [test](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/rule.test/test):

- **≤11:** Your information reaches no one.
- **12-16:** Your information reaches the nearest populated area of town [size](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/rule.character/size) or larger. You and each ally present when you make the [test](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/rule.test/test) gain an [edge](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/rule.dice/edge) on [Presence](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/rule.character/presence) [tests](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/rule.test/test) in that area until one of you spends a [Recovery](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/rule.health/recoveries).
- **17+:** Your information reaches the nearest populated area of town [size](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/rule.character/size) or larger, plus the next closest such population. You and allies present for your [test](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/rule.test/test) gain an [edge](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/rule.dice/edge) on [Presence](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/rule.character/presence) [tests](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/rule.test/test) made in those areas until you start your next [respite](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/rule.resource/respite).
