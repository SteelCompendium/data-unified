| Doctrine   | Triggered Action |
|------------|------------------|
| Insurgent  | [Advanced Tactics](scc:mcdm.heroes.v1/feature.ability.tactician.level-1/advanced-tactics) |

| Vanguard   | [Parry](scc:mcdm.heroes.v1/feature.ability.tactician.level-1/parry)            |
