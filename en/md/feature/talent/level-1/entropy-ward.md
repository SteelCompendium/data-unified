Your ward slows time for your enemies. Whenever a creature deals damage to you, their [speed](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/rule.character/speed) is reduced by an amount equal to your [Reason](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/rule.character/reason) score and they can't use [triggered actions](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/rule.combat/triggered-action) until the end of their next [turn](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/rule.combat/turn).
