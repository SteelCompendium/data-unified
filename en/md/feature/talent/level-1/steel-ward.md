Your ward reacts to danger, protecting you from future harm. Whenever you take damage, after the damage resolves, you gain [damage immunity](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/rule.damage/damage-immunity) equal to your [Reason](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/rule.character/reason) score until the end of your next [turn](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/rule.combat/turn).
