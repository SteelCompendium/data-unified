A hero with a [martial](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/culture/martial) upbringing was raised by warriors. These might have been the soldiers of an established army, a band of mercenaries, a guild of monster-slaying adventurers, or any other folk whose lives revolve around combat. Heroes with a [martial](scc.v1:mcdm.heroes.v1/culture/martial) upbringing are always ready for a fight—and they know how to finish that fight.
