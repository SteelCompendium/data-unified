*This thick red liquid smells of cinnamon.*

**Keywords:** Magic, Potion

**[Item Prerequisite](scc:mcdm.heroes.v1/rule.downtime/item-prerequisite):** An ounce of the blood of Saint Elspeth

**[Project Source](scc:mcdm.heroes.v1/rule.downtime/project-source):** Texts or lore in Caelian

**[Project Roll](scc:mcdm.heroes.v1/rule.downtime/project-roll) [Characteristic](scc:mcdm.heroes.v1/rule.character/characteristic):** [Presence](scc:mcdm.heroes.v1/rule.character/presence)

**Project Goal:** 360

**Effect:** When you pour the elixir onto your forehead as a maneuver, it vanishes and you protect yourself against effects that might harm your body, mind, or soul. For a number of rounds equal to your current [Victories](scc:mcdm.heroes.v1/rule.resource/victories), any enemy ability targeting you automatically obtains a tier 1 outcome against you. Additionally, the ability can only deal damage to you, letting you ignore its other effects.
